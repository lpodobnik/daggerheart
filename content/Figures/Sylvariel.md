# Sylvariel the Dragon

> The dragon of Elaruun, behind the winds.

**Avatar of [[Sylvanus]]** · appeared c. 2,000 years before year zero (First Age), on [[Elaruun]]

## Story
Sylvariel appeared on Elaruun in the same years Sylvanus made the [[Water Elves]]. Then the dragon moved among the peoples:
- **c. 1,000 years before year zero** turned a party of [[Explorers]] into the secluded, xenophobic [[Last Tide]]
- **c. year 100** slipped among the [[Lizardmen]] and founded the [[Arcane Fang]], usurpers of magic
- **c. year 500** founded the [[Elf Outpost]] on the coast between the jungle's edge and the mountains
- **c. year 600** built the [[Twin City]] to control the pass between two mountains
- **c. year 630** decided the elves could no longer stay apart, and raised a water elf army against the lizardmen
