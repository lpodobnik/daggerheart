> A woman made of flame: "the First Born of the Fire", bathing in her volcano.

**Avatar of [[Svarog]]** · appeared c. 3,000 years before year zero (First Age), answering Atlas's Talus

## Story
Ardora lives inside the volcano [[Ardor]]. When it erupted, the glowing stones that fell became the [[Fire Genasi]]. Their nobility, [[Ardora's First]], claim descent from the very first of them. Around year 100 she founded [[Cinderforge]], the Fire Genasi city on the northern island. Around year 640, when the city's crystal was cracking, she had Cinderforge's scholars ring it with the Pillars of Magic Harnessing, tall coils that catch the leaking magic and feed it to the city.

## Now
She is in her volcano and cannot be reached.
