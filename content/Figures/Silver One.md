> A silver shape risen out of Star Lake: humanoid before there were humanoids, with no face and no sex.

**Avatar of [[Astralis]]** · appeared c. 1,000 years before year zero, in the First Age

## Story
One day a shape rose out of [[Star Lake]]. It was humanoid, though no humanoids existed yet, silver, faceless and sexless. It wandered into the [[Frozen North]] and was snowed on.

Whereabouts unknown.
