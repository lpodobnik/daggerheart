> An empty suit of obsidian plate that stood up out of the desert sands.

**Avatar of [[Atlas]]** · appeared c. year 200 (Second Age) · first called **the Doombringer**

## Story
He rose from the sands of the [[Obsidian Desert]]. Around year 500 he raised the [[Orcs]] a second army. Around year 600 he led two orc armies and their war beasts against [[Glaive Port]] and took it ([[Fall of Glaive Port]]). There was no massacre and no pillage: curfew, martial law, and a trading city still trading. The city became the heart of a [[Great Orkish Empire]] in the making.

The occupiers rotted into brutes. Around year 610, disgusted that his war had decayed into corruption, he **abandoned the orcs** and went to the [[Harpies]]. Under him their [[Sisters of Battle]] became holy warriors, sworn to fight injustice everywhere. Around year 630 he raised a [[Lizardmen|lizardman]] army on [[Jungle Island]] and built them a second war camp, and the lizardmen split into two warring factions.

## Now
He leads the harpies' Sisters of Battle.
