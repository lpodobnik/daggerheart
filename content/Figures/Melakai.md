> A leper of Glaive Port who healed himself, and then began to heal others.

**Avatar of [[Svarog]]** · appeared c. year 630 (Third Age)

## Story
Melakai was a leper in plague-ridden [[Glaive Port]]. He healed himself and began spreading healing to others. In those same years he founded the [[Mycomancers]], who command the [[Fungal Zombies]] and became the city's ruling class. Around year 640 he raised Glaive Port a defensive army, and the city held against the [[Deepkin]] in the endless [[Siege of Glaive Port]].

## Now
He is old and decrepit, but alive, and lives in Glaive Port.

## Connections
- [[Mycomancers]]: his order, Glaive Port's real rulers
- [[Glaive Port]]: his city
- [[Fungal Plague]]: rose from the plague-ridden city
