# The Echoes of the Void

> They sense that sound, dance and colour together can reach their ancestors — but do not yet know how.

**Founded: c. 1,000 years before year zero (First Age), by [[Astralis]], with the Trenchborn**

## Story
As [[Astralis]] walked his coloured shore, strange beings came out of the sea: tentacles where the lower half of a skull should be, skin iridescent like the inside of a shell; few, and reproducing by rituals not even they fully understand. Other races call them the [[Trenchborn]], and they are evil. Their order, the Echoes of the Void, senses that sound, dance and colour together can reach their ancestors — whatever that means.

## Now
The Trenchborn were untouched in the later Ages and remain a mystery.

## Connections
- [[Trenchborn]] — their people
- [[Coloured Sands]] — the shore they came from
