> The first order of the water elves, who mean to find what lies on far unknown shores.

**Founded: c. 2,000 years before year zero (First Age), by [[Sylvanus]], with the Water Elves**

## Story
In the same years that [[Sylvariel]] appeared on [[Elaruun]], [[Sylvanus]] made the [[Water Elves]]: skilled and neutral, spreading through the islands along the cold currents, [[Astralis]]'s bamboo letting them build rafts. Their first order were the Explorers. Late in the First Age, c. 1,000 years before year zero, a party of Explorers who reached a far place turned inward and became secluded, evil xenophobes — the [[Last Tide]].

## Now
Nothing has been heard of them since the Last Tide split away.

## Connections
- [[Water Elves]] — their people
