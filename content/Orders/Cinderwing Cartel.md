> Fire Genasi who built steam-driven airships to explore the world — and, grounded, became masters of portals.

**Founded: c. year 100 (Second Age), by [[Svarog]], in [[Cinderforge]]**

## Story
Once the [[Fire Genasi]] discovered engineering and steam power, [[Svarog]] founded the Cinderwing Cartel in Cinderforge: Fire Genasi who build steam-driven airships and set out to explore the world. In the same years he commanded them to build a **vast trade network across the continent** — and to see off any competition.

Around year 200, trading far and wide, they reached the island amid the ice floes between [[Jungle Island]] and the mainland, where after a mutiny on an airship a splinter renounced fire and became the [[Frost Genasi]], on bad terms with the Cartel's centre. Later in those same years [[Sylvanus]]'s [[Skybreak]] closed the sky, and nothing may fly.

Late in Age II, c. year 500, Svarog sent the grounded Cartel a prophecy: one of the two lost crystals governs **portals**, and they were to learn everything of portals, other dimensions and time. Then he made them masters of it: they found that shards of the [[First Meteor]] — the one that made [[World's Breach]] — make portal magic far stronger nearby; far from the shards it is almost impossible.

## Now
Grounded, chasing portals and meteor shards. They know the shards' secret.
