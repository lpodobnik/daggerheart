> The nobility of fire: those descended from the very first Genasi to fall burning from Ardor.

**Founded: c. 3,000 years before year zero (First Age), by [[Svarog]], with the Fire Genasi themselves**

## Story
[[Svarog]] would not be outdone by the dwarves: [[Ardor]] erupted, and the glowing stones that fell were quickened by the chaos-energy of the cosmos into the [[Fire Genasi]] — evil, and also claiming the name Children of Stone. Obsessed with bloodline, they keep a small order of those descended from the very first: Ardora's First, their nobility. The name ties them to [[Ardora]], the woman of flame who lives in the volcano.

## Now
Nothing has been heard of them since. The Fire Genasi now live in [[Cinderforge]], harnessing their cracked crystal with the Pillars of Magic Harnessing.
