> The first human order: pure survival and peace, from a people who had met no one yet.

**Founded: year zero (Second Age), by [[Astralis]], with the Humans**

## Story
After the [[Second Meteor]], [[Astralis]] concluded that the only way to multiply the faithful was the opposite of everything he had made so far: the most basic, featureless beings — but many, who breed easily and gladly and spread. They named themselves [[Humans]]: neutral, born on the [[Great Plains]] along the [[River of Life]]. Their first order was one of pure survival and peace, the Cradle, founded before they had met anyone else.

## Now
Nothing has been heard of them since. Humans are now the most numerous people and the dominant city-builders.
