# The Mages' Guild

> To study magic, write it all down, and buy up everything strange.

**Founded: c. year 630 (Third Age), by [[Svarog]], at the [[Bazaar]]**

## Story
After [[Atlas]]'s [[Shattering]] broke one great crystal and cracked the rest, [[Svarog]] — delighted by the chaos and annoyed not to understand it — founded a human order, the Mages' Guild, at the Bazaar: to study magic, write it all down, and buy up crystal shards, meteor fragments and curiosities.

## Now
The Guild buys shards and curiosities at the Bazaar.

## Connections
- [[Bazaar]] — their base
- [[Great Crystals]] · [[Shattering]] — what they study
- [[Meteors]] — fragments they collect
- [[Cinderwing Cartel]] — the other order after meteor shards
