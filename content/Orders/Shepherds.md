> Samaritans of many races, bound to no city, who give up their possessions to help wherever there is ruin. They own nothing and give everything; the refugees they lead call them the Shepherds.

**Founded: c. year 610 (Third Age), by [[Atlas]]**

## Story
Around year 610, the time of [[Elyndra's Defeat]] and [[Glaive Port]]'s cannibalism, [[Atlas]] founded an order of samaritans: many races, bound to no city, who give up their possessions to help wherever there is ruin.

## Now
Nothing has been heard of them since — but ruin is everywhere: the plague, the [[Wasteland]], the refugees at the [[Bazaar]].
