> Winged holy warriors, sworn to fight injustice everywhere.

**Founded: c. year 400 (Second Age), by [[Atlas]] · made holy warriors c. year 610 (Third Age), under the [[Hollow Knight]]**

## Story
[[Atlas]] made the [[Harpies]] in the south of [[Sky Spear]] — winged bird-women, good — and with them their order, the Sisters of Battle.

Around year 610, in Age III, the [[Hollow Knight]], disgusted that his war at [[Glaive Port]] had decayed into corruption, abandoned the [[Orcs|orcs]] and went to the harpies. Under him the Sisters of Battle became holy warriors, sworn to fight injustice everywhere.

## Now
Holy warriors against injustice, with the Hollow Knight at their head.
