# The Elf Outpost Marines

> Fighters for war at sea, raised at the Elf Outpost.

**Founded: c. year 620 (Third Age), by [[Astralis]], in [[Sylvanus]]'s [[Elf Outpost]]**

## Story
The Elf Outpost, founded by [[Sylvariel]] on the coast between the jungle's edge and the mountains late in Age II (c. year 500), founded an order of marines — fighters for water combat.

## Now
The Outpost is known for its marines. The water elves as a whole have been corrupted into decadence, and they hold one Underdark mouth on [[Jungle Island]].
