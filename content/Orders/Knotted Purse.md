> Halfling moneylenders of the roads, who keep their debts in knots.

**Founded: c. year 500 (late in the Second Age), by [[Atlas]], with the Halflings**

## Story
[[Atlas]] saw the [[Centaur Roads]] crowded and the sky closed by the [[Skybreak]], and filled the gap with a people who belong nowhere and everywhere: the [[Halflings]], small nomad traders of the roads, neutral. Among them, the Knotted Purse, an order of moneylenders.

## The cords
A nomad keeps no ledger. Every debt owed to the Knotted Purse is tied as a knot on a cord the lender wears — the kind and colour of the knot tell the sum, the terms and the debtor. A lender's cords are his whole fortune and his whole memory, and he never takes them off. When a debt is paid, the knot is untied in front of the debtor; among halflings, *untying the knot* means settling any account, and a man whose knot is still tied is a man not to be trusted.

Because the cords are the only record, a lost or stolen cord is a catastrophe: every debt on it becomes a debt no one can prove — and whoever holds the cord holds the debtors.

## Now
The Knotted Purse lends money at the [[Bazaar]], the one safe crossroads left, drowning in refugees.
