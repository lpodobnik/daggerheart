> Mushroom-eating shamans who build non-Euclidean structures; whoever sees one goes absolutely insane.

**Founded: c. year 100 (Second Age), by [[Astralis]], with the Deepkin**

## Story
In [[Fungorn]] [[Astralis]] raised a new people from the mixing of dwarf explorers and Trenchborn explorers: short and stout, with bearded tentacles — the [[Deepkin]]. Their shamans, the Bent Eye, eat the mushrooms and build non-Euclidean structures no one has yet seen; the story goes that whoever sees one goes absolutely insane.

In Age III part of Fungorn was burned (c. year 610), and the Deepkin, true mushroom-folk and immune to the [[Fungal Plague]], raised an army (c. year 630) and laid endless siege to [[Glaive Port]] (c. year 640).

## Now
Nothing has been heard of them by name since their founding. Their people besiege Glaive Port without end.
