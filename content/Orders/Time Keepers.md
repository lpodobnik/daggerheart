> Women-led centaur sky-watchers who drew the first maps of the world and made the first calendars.

**Founded: c. year 100 (Second Age), by [[Astralis]], among [[Atlas]]'s Centaurs**

## Story
[[Astralis]] went among [[Atlas]]'s [[Centaurs]] and made the Time Keepers: a women-led order who found that by watching the sky they could align their cycles and rituals. Watching the night sky, they drew the first maps of the world and made the first calendars. They date all time from the [[Second Meteor]]: **year zero is the falling star that landed far in the west with a great roar.**

## Now
Nothing has been heard of them since. Year zero is the second meteor.
