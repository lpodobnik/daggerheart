# Atlas's First

> The lizardman nobility — each of whom became noble by killing every sibling from his own clutch of eggs.

**Founded: c. year 100 (Second Age), by [[Atlas]], with the Lizardmen**

## Story
[[Atlas]] made the [[Lizardmen]] in [[Sylvanus]]'s jungles on the southern island: evil, wholly tribal, ruled by Atlas's First — the nobles, each of whom became so by killing every sibling from his own clutch of eggs. Around year 200 Sylvanus gave them their "city," a chain of jungle villages.

In Age III the lizardmen took up arms (c. year 630, Sylvanus), the [[Hollow Knight]] raised them a second army, and Sylvanus sent them through the [[Underdark]] against the [[Tree of Life]]. The [[Grove]]'s misdirection held, its assassins killed their chiefs, and the lizardmen turned on each other; the Hollow Knight built them a second war camp and they split into two warring factions around the Underdark mouth. Around year 640 [[Elyndra]] and the water elves took that mouth; the lizardmen were broken but not destroyed.

## Now
The lizardmen are split in two and at war with each other on [[Jungle Island]]; the elves hold one Underdark mouth, the lizardmen the other.
