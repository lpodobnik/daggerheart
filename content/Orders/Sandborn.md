# The Sandborn

> The orcs' order of warriors, born in the desert for glory and war.

**Founded: c. year 200 (Second Age), by [[Atlas]], with the Orcs**

## Story
[[Atlas]] raised the [[Orcs]] in the [[Obsidian Desert]] — living for glory and war — with their order of warriors, the Sandborn.

The orcs went on to become masters of war beasts (c. year 300), raised armies in the desert, and founded the [[Orc War Camps]] (c. year 400). In Age III the [[Hollow Knight]] led two orc armies to take [[Glaive Port]]; the occupiers rotted there, while the orcs who stayed in the desert said *that is not the way* and fused the [[Desert Crystals|desert crystals]] into a great obelisk, [[Final End|the Final End]].

## Now
Nothing has been heard of them by name since their founding. The desert orcs are "good but fanatical"; their camps have no armies left.
