# The Vengeful Paladins

> *The only good orc is a dead orc.*

**Founded: c. year 610 (Third Age), by [[Svarog]] · evil in practice · "…of the Righteous Suffering"**

## Story
Among [[Glaive Port]]'s refugees in other cities rose a fanatical, religious order of vengeful paladins, a god of suffering at its heart, recruiting among the poor and sworn to kill [[Orcs|orcs]]. It believes itself the ultimate good and is, in practice, evil.

## Now
Nothing has been heard of them since. Glaive Port is still orc-held in name, and refugees pour into the [[Bazaar]].
