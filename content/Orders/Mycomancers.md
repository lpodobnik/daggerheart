> They command the fungal dead — and the dead now patrol Glaive Port's streets without harming anyone.

**Founded: c. year 630 (Third Age), by [[Svarog]] through [[Melakai]] · Evil**

## Story
In plague-ridden [[Glaive Port]] a leper healed himself and began to heal others: [[Melakai]] the Messenger, Svarog's avatar. In those same years he founded the Mycomancers — humans, orcs, anyone — magic users who command the [[Fungal Zombies|fungal dead]]. They became Glaive Port's ruling class. The [[Deepkin]], true mushroom-folk, held raising corpses with fungus a travesty and raised an army.

Some ten years later, around year 640, Melakai raised Glaive Port a defensive army, the Deepkin laid siege, and the city held: a siege without end ([[Siege of Glaive Port]]). Then Mycomancer agents slipped out of Glaive Port disguised as fungus, to seed hidden cells in other cities — evil, wanting to spread the fungus everywhere.

## Now
Orcs are Glaive Port's nominal rulers; the Mycomancers the real ones, through religion. The fungal dead patrol the streets. Their cells hide in other cities — perhaps the [[Bazaar]].
