# Divine Strike

> Harpies who spot for the Ballista from the air.

**Founded: c. year 620 (Third Age), by [[Atlas]], with the Harpies**

## Story
When [[Heart of the Plains]] raised the [[Ballista Tower]] — too big to aim, with a range of half the continent ([[Astralis]]) — [[Atlas]]'s [[Harpies]] founded Divine Strike to spot for it from the air.

## Now
At the end of the Age the [[Third Meteor]] struck the slopes beside the Ballista's city: quake and mudslides broke the city and cracked the Ballista, now out of action.

## Connections
- [[Ballista Tower]] — the weapon they aim
- [[Harpies]] — their people
- [[Sisters of Battle]] — the harpies' holy warriors
- [[Third Meteor]] — broke the Ballista
