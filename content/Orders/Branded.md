# The Branded

> Pirates, prisoners and dock-scum, necks burned black with hot tar, who defend Glaive Port by the dirtiest means there are.

**Founded: c. year 500 (late in the Second Age), by [[Astralis]], in [[Glaive Port]]**

## Story
In Glaive Port, in a time of fear, the ruler gathered pirates, prisoners and the worst scum of the docks into the Branded — their necks burned black with hot tar. They defend the city's river and sea by the dirtiest means there are: fire-ships full of people rammed into the enemy, plague corpses over the walls.

Through the Third Age their city was taken by the orcs ([[Fall of Glaive Port]]), corrupted, besieged by [[Elyndra]], driven to cannibalism and famine, and finally ruled by the [[Mycomancers]].

## Now
The Branded still hold Glaive Port's waters, loyal, through the endless [[Siege of Glaive Port]].
