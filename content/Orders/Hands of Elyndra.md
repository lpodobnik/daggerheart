# The Hands of Elyndra

> Ice Giant healers who learned, through the crystal of the north, to channel healing magic.

**Founded: c. year 100 (Second Age), by [[Sylvanus]], with the Ice Giants**

## Story
Out of the [[Frozen North]] [[Sylvanus]] brought the [[Ice Giants]] — good, and healers — whose order, the Hands of Elyndra, learned through the crystal there to channel healing magic. (The order is older than its namesake: [[Elyndra]] only appeared c. year 400, in Age II.)

Around year 610, in Age III, Elyndra led the Healers and the [[Purifiers of Evil]] to liberate [[Glaive Port]] and was decisively defeated; her army was destroyed and the battlefield became the [[Corpse Field]] of the Ice Giants ([[Elyndra's Defeat]]). Some ten years later, around year 620, what remained of the giants withdrew to [[Coldspire]], now rare, with few healers left.

## Now
Few healers remain, among the last Ice Giants in Coldspire. The northern crystal near Coldspire is cracked.

## Connections
- [[Ice Giants]] — their people
- [[Elyndra]] — their namesake and war-leader
- [[Great Crystals]] — the Frozen North crystal, source of their healing
- [[Coldspire]] — refuge of the last giants
- [[Purifiers of Evil]] — marched and fell beside them
