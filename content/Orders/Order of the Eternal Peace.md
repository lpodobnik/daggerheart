# The Order of the Eternal Peace

> Sworn to keep the Eternal Alliance from ever breaking — by any means necessary.

**Founded: c. year 500 (late in the Second Age), by [[Atlas]], among [[Astralis]]'s shapeshifting centaurs**

## Story
[[Astralis]] bound [[Humans|humans]] and [[Centaurs|centaurs]] in the [[Eternal Alliance]], signed in the central city for shared prosperity and defence, and from it came a new, good sub-race: the [[Shapeshifting Centaurs|shapeshifting centaurs]], who by ritual and magic can take human form for a while. [[Atlas]] founded among them the Order of the Eternal Peace, sworn to keep the alliance from ever breaking, by any means necessary.

Around year 600, at the start of the Third Age, the order, through the [[Stone Talus]], called humans and centaurs to arms in [[Greenhaven]] (Atlas).

## Now
Nothing has been heard of them since. The alliance's humans and centaurs fight the water elves without end in the [[War Zone]].
