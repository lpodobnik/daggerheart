> The first warriors of the Children of Stone, armed with crude stone weapons.

**Founded: c. 3,000 years before year zero (First Age), by [[Atlas]] — the first order in the world**

## Story
When [[Atlas]] commanded the [[Stone Talus]], lightning struck it from the tip of [[Sky Spear]] and its fallen pieces became the proto-[[Dwarves|dwarves]], who went to live inside the mountain. Because Atlas is a god of war, a sect of warriors with crude stone weapons arose among them: the Stone Defenders.

## Now
Nothing has been heard of them since their founding. The dwarves themselves have since walled [[Kharag Duun]] twice over, raised armies, and taken [[Greenhaven]].

## Connections
- [[Dwarves]] — the people they defend

- [[Sky Spear]] — their mountain home
