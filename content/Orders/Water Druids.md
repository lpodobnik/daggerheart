# The Water Druids

> The Grove's defenders: they confuse armies until their generals vanish, eaten by fungus or by trees.

**Founded: c. year 620 (Third Age), by [[Sylvanus]], at the [[Grove]]**

## Story
The [[Grove]], the elves' hidden city around the [[Tree of Life]], turned to magical defence — no walls, but a forest that shifts and scouts who walk in circles. Then [[Sylvanus]] raised there an army of water druids.

In those same years [[Glaive Port]]'s four armies marched south for bread against the Grove: the [[Great Battle of the Third Age]]. The druids confused them, their generals vanished — eaten by fungus or by trees — and with the [[Spider People]] they drove them out; the forest swallowed them. The rest scattered as bandits across the [[Wasteland]], and the Grove survived, wounded.

## Now
The Grove survives, hidden from every race.
