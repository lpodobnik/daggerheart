# The Purifiers of Evil

> A good Ice Giant order raised for a counter-offensive — to take an army and set Glaive Port free.

**Founded: c. year 600 (Third Age), by [[Sylvanus]] through [[Elyndra]] · Good**

## Story
When the [[Hollow Knight]] took [[Glaive Port]] ([[Fall of Glaive Port]]), [[Elyndra]] answered by founding among the [[Ice Giants]] a good order for a counter-offensive: the Purifiers of Evil.

Some ten years later, around year 610, Elyndra led the Healers ([[Hands of Elyndra]]) and the Purifiers to liberate Glaive Port and was decisively defeated. Her army was destroyed and she escaped; the pursuers burned part of [[Fungorn]], the river was rerouted and the lake began to dry, and the battlefield became the [[Corpse Field]] of the Ice Giants ([[Elyndra's Defeat]]). The siege had lasted six or seven years; the giants had frozen the lake, and fishing and trade died.

## Now
What remained of the Ice Giants withdrew to [[Coldspire]], now rare.
