> Centaur priests who hold that the union of beast and man makes them superior to every other race.

**Founded: year zero (Second Age), by [[Atlas]], with the Centaurs · Evil**

## Story
[[Atlas]] made the [[Centaurs]] on the [[Eternal Steppe]], which is perfect for riding: neutral nomads, but with a small evil order of priests, the Purebred, supremacists who believe their blend of beast and man sets them above all others.

Around year 300, in Age II, [[Sylvanus]] purified the centaurs.

## Now
Nothing has been heard of them since. The centaurs keep the [[Centaur Roads]] and the [[Eternal Alliance]] with humans.
