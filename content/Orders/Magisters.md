# The Magisters

> The dwarves are not only the first race but the best — and the Magisters will do anything to stay in power.

**Founded: c. year 640 (Third Age), by [[Atlas]] · Evil, power-hungry**

## Story
Seeing [[Humans|humans]] become the dominant race, the [[Dwarves|dwarves]] marched with the [[Stone Talus]] and took [[Greenhaven]] as its wardens — no massacre, nothing else changed. (They had already, around year 610, declared themselves the first and best of races and walled [[Kharag Duun]] twice over.) In those same years they became master diplomats. [[Karros and Kassander]] came home from the front, found the dwarves ruling Greenhaven, and turned them to the honourable way ([[Astralis]]). But Atlas raised among those same dwarves a ruling caste, the Magisters, evil and power-hungry.

## Now
Greenhaven is dwarf-ruled — purified to the honourable way by Karros and Kassander, yet governed by the Magisters. Which wins remains to be seen.
