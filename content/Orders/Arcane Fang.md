# The Arcane Fang

> Lizardman usurpers of magic who want every crystal for their sons.

**Founded: c. year 100 (Second Age), by [[Sylvanus]] through [[Sylvariel]]**

## Story
In the same years that [[Atlas]] made the [[Lizardmen]], [[Sylvariel]] slipped among them and founded a sect of usurpers of magic who want every crystal for their sons: the Arcane Fang — a Sylvanus order planted inside an Atlas people.

## Now
Nothing has been heard of them since. The lizardmen are split into two warring factions on [[Jungle Island]], and the [[Great Crystals]] are cracked or shattered.

## Connections
- [[Lizardmen]] — the people they live among
- [[Sylvariel]] — their founder
- [[Great Crystals]] — what they covet
