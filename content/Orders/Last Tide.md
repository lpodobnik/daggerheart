> Water elves who went too far, turned inward, and want only to be left alone — and then to rule the seas.

**Founded: c. 1,000 years before year zero (First Age), by [[Sylvanus]] through [[Sylvariel]] · Evil · City: [[Nerathis]]**

## Story
Late in the First Age, c. 1,000 years before year zero, [[Sylvariel]] moved among the elves: a party of [[Explorers]] who reached a far place turned inward and became secluded xenophobes — evil — who want only to be left alone.

**Age II.** [[Sylvanus]] founded a first city for them, [[Nerathis]] (in the first years after year zero). Around year 300 [[Svarog]] corrupted it, and Sylvanus advanced it into a shipyard of **warships of doom**, for the secluded elves meant to rule the seas.

**Age III.** Around year 600 their warships raided every settlement along the rich northern shores and took a foothold (Sylvanus); the [[Fire Genasi]] raised an army as the elven ships sailed past their island. Sylvanus then bound corrupted [[Glaive Port]] to them in the [[Evil Alliance]]. Around year 610 generations of inbreeding cost them a whole generation's shipbuilding ([[Astralis]]): their raiders are now small fast boats of twenty, and for a while there will be no new ones. Around year 630 [[Atlas]]'s [[Shattering]] broke the great crystal on their island — chosen, it is said, by chance — and the surge spread [[Monstrosities|monstrosities]]. Around year 640 Svarog corrupted the whole water elf civilisation into arrogance and decadence.

## Now
Their island holds the shattered crystal: wild magic and monstrosities, densest here. A decadent, corrupted elf navy that can't build new ships for a generation — decadent, rebuilding.
