> Dissonant, terrible choral music set down from the void's sound, that stops a listener where they stand.

**Founded: c. year 300 (Second Age), by [[Astralis]], with the Humans · Neutral**

## Story
When [[Astralis]] opened a ley-line portal near the [[Twins]] — the [[Void Glance]] — those far enough away heard it only as sound. Among them a band of [[Humans]] found patterns in what they had heard and set them down as hymns: dissonant, terrible choral music that stops a listener where they stand. They became the Star Chorus, and their mission is to teach it to anyone who wishes to learn.

## Now
Nothing has been heard of them since.
