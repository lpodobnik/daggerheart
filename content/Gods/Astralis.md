# Astralis

> God of "Arts and Stars": star-shaped lakes, songs that stun, and worshippers by any means.

**Agenda:** worshippers, by any means.

## Story
Astralis filled the First Age with strange beauty: [[Star Lake]], the [[Obsidian Spires]], the [[Coloured Sands]] ("and there is a reason for it"), and [[Star Metal]] hidden in [[Sky Spear]]. To multiply the faithful he did the opposite of everything he had made so far and made the [[Humans]]: the most basic, featureless beings, but *many*. He tore a glimpse into the void (the [[Void Glance]]) and bound humans and centaurs in the [[Eternal Alliance]]. In the Third Age he raised the [[Ballista Tower]], set the dead walking ([[Fungal Zombies]]), and, angry that [[Atlas]] would suppress magic, collapsed the [[Left Eye of Atlas]].
