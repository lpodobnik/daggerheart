> God of earth and war, a "heart of conflict" that softened at the sight of life, then broke magic to keep war honest.

**Agenda:** war as steel on steel. He broke the crystals to take magic out of it.

## Story
In the First Age Atlas shaped the land's bones: a hand, a spear, two eyes and a fortress of stone. Watching what the [[Stone Talus]] had made and what [[Astralis]] had made, he felt his heart of conflict soften, and gave the plains the [[River of Life]]. In the Second Age he filled the world with peoples. In the Third the [[Hollow Knight]] took [[Glaive Port]]. Then he grew disgusted twice. First the occupation rotted into corruption, so he walked away from the [[Orcs]]. Then war turned into "druids and misdirection instead of steel on steel", and he poured all his power into the [[Shattering]]. Astralis, angry that he would suppress magic, struck him in the eye (the [[Collapse of the Left Eye]]).

- c. 3,000 years before year zero — [[Stone Talus]]; struck him with lightning to make the [[Dwarves]] and the [[Stone Defenders]]
- c. 2,000 years before year zero — [[Obsidian Desert]]; [[Kharag Duun]]; [[River of Life]]
- c. 1,000 years before year zero — [[Twins]]; [[Left Eye of Atlas]]; [[First Fortress]]

**Age II: the Coming of the Races**
- year zero — [[Centaurs]] and the [[Purebred]]
- c. year 100 — dwarves meld crystal magic with [[Star Metal]]; [[Lizardmen]] and [[Atlas's First]]; the [[Magical Forge]] (via the Talus)
- c. year 200 — the Doombringer ([[Hollow Knight]]); [[Orcs]] and the [[Sandborn]]
- c. year 300 — orcs become masters of war beasts; an army in the south-eastern desert; [[Coldspire]]
- c. year 400 — [[Harpies]] and the [[Sisters of Battle]]; the [[Orc War Camps]]; the Library in [[Greenhaven]]
- c. year 500 — [[Halflings]] and the [[Kamatari]]; a second orc army (Doombringer); the [[Order of the Eternal Peace]]; the [[Bazaar]] (via the Talus)

**Age III: the Age of War**
- c. year 600 — the Order of the Eternal Peace arms Greenhaven; the [[Fall of Glaive Port]] and the [[Great Orkish Empire]]; the [[Final End]]
- c. year 610 — the Hollow Knight leaves the orcs for the [[Sisters of Battle]]; the [[Vow of Peace]]; the Great Wall of Kharag Duun; the Bazaar made untouchable
- c. year 620 — [[Divine Strike]]; a dwarf army
- c. year 630 — a lizardman army and a second war camp (Hollow Knight); the [[Shattering]]; another dwarf army (Talus)
- c. year 640 — the dwarves take Greenhaven; dwarves become master diplomats; the [[Magisters]]
