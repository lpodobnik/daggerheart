> The god of the wild who raised islands, brought magic into the world, and built a great many cities.

**Agenda:** life spreading outward, and lately war.

## Story
Sylvanus raised the world's first land out of the western sea, [[Sylmara Archipelago|Sylmara]], and ringed it with winds. He changed the world at the start of the Second Age: floating crystals appeared in four places, and **magic entered the world** ([[Coming of Magic]]). He closed the sky ([[Skybreak]]) and opened the [[Underdark]]. In the Third Age his elves and giants went to war. [[Elyndra]] was broken at [[Glaive Port]], but the hidden [[Grove]] around his [[Tree of Life]] survived the [[Great Battle of the Third Age]]. At the end of the Age he hid the [[Lost City]], which may know where the lost crystals lie.

- c. 4,000 years before year zero — [[Gorge of the Titans]]; jungle on the southern island
- c. 3,000 years before year zero — two mountain lakes and a great delta
- c. 2,000 years before year zero — [[Sylvariel]]; the [[Water Elves]] and the [[Explorers]]; all of [[Jungle Island]]
- c. 1,000 years before year zero — [[Frozen North]]; [[Nyravel]]; the [[Last Tide]] (via Sylvariel)

**Age II: the Coming of the Races**
- year zero — the [[Coming of Magic]] ([[Great Crystals]]); [[Nerathis]]
- c. year 100 — [[Ice Giants]] and the [[Hands of Elyndra]]; the [[Arcane Fang]] (via Sylvariel)
- c. year 200 — the lizardfolk's jungle villages; the [[Skybreak]]; the [[Underdark]]
- c. year 300 — purified the [[Centaurs]]; [[Greenhaven]]; Nerathis's warships of doom
- c. year 400 — [[Elyndra]]; [[Selvaruun]] on [[Elaruun]]; the [[Settlers]]
- c. year 500 — [[Desert Crystals]], and the two lost crystals admitted; Elyndra readies the Ice Giants; the [[Elf Outpost]] (via Sylvariel)

**Age III: the Age of War**
- c. year 600 — the Last Tide raid the north shores; the [[Twin City]] (Sylvariel); the [[Purifiers of Evil]] (Elyndra); the [[Evil Alliance]]
- c. year 610 — the [[Tree of Life]]; [[Elyndra's Defeat]] and the [[Corpse Field]]; the [[Grove]]
- c. year 620 — [[Elyndra's Coastal City]]; the Grove's magical defence; the [[Water Druids]]
- c. year 630 — the [[Lizardmen]] take up arms and strike through the Underdark at the Tree; a water elf army (Sylvariel)
- c. year 640 — the [[Lost City]]; Elyndra's army takes Jungle Island's Underdark mouth
