> "I'm the god of chaos." Fire, the forge, and all three stones from the sky.

**Agenda:** "My plan is to have no plan."

## Story
Svarog let chaos choose his place. He struck the continent with something from the sky ([[First Meteor]]), dropped a second on the dwarves as they entered the [[First Fortress]] ([[Second Meteor]]), and let chaos choose where the third would fall ([[Third Meteor]]). He made fire-folk and raiders. He also founded a good human order, the [[Flaming Fist]], and no one knows why. In the Third Age he took [[Glaive Port]] down every dark road: cannibalism, famine, plague, the [[Mycomancers]]. Delighted by the chaos of the [[Shattering]] and annoyed not to understand it, he founded the [[Mages' Guild]].

## Works
**Age I: the Shaping**
- c. 6,000 years before year zero — the [[First Meteor]] and [[World's Breach]]; the arid desert around it
- c. 5,000 years before year zero — [[Slithering River]] from a spring in [[Sky Spear]]; [[Sea of Ice]], its glacier and the warm sea
- c. 4,000 years before year zero — [[Long Harbour]]; [[Ardor]]
- c. 3,000 years before year zero — [[Ardora]]; Ardor erupts, making the [[Fire Genasi]] and [[Ardora's First]]
- c. 2,000 years before year zero — [[Fungorn]]; [[Black Mesa]]; the [[Fiery Land]]
- c. 1,000 years before year zero — [[Badlands]]; [[Svarog Glacier]]; [[Misty Mountains]]

**Age II: the Coming of the Races**
- year zero — the [[Second Meteor]] on the First Fortress
- c. year 100 — [[Cinderforge]] (via Ardora); Fire Genasi engineering and steam power; the [[Cinderwing Cartel]] and its trade network
- c. year 200 — [[Frost Genasi]]; [[Goblins]] and the [[Marrow Munchers]]
- c. year 300 — [[Skjaldborg]]; corrupted [[Nerathis]]
- c. year 400 — the [[Flaming Fist]]; [[Glaive Port]] (commanded the humans), then made it a defensive fortress
- c. year 500 — an army for Glaive Port; the Cartel's prophecy of [[Portals]], then mastery of them; the [[Frost Genasi City]]

**Age III: the Age of War**
- c. year 600 — a Genasi army on their island; the Marrow Munchers' tunnels
- c. year 610 — the [[Vengeful Paladins]]; Glaive Port corrupted to cannibalism (his avatar)
- c. year 620 — the [[Fungal Plague]]; another Glaive Port army; famine and a fourth army, sent south to the [[Great Battle of the Third Age]]
- c. year 630 — [[Malakai]]; the [[Mycomancers]]; the [[Mages' Guild]]
- c. year 640 — Malakai's defensive army; the Pillars of Magic Harnessing at Cinderforge (via Ardora); Mycomancer agents sent out; the [[Water Elves]] corrupted into decadence; the [[Third Meteor]]
