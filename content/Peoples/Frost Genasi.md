> Genasi who renounced fire, embraced ice, and lost their cinder to the cold.

**Created:** c. year 200 (Second Age), by [[Svarog]] · a sub-race of the [[Fire Genasi]]

## Story
The [[Cinderwing Cartel]], trading far and wide, came to the island amid the ice floes that bridge [[Jungle Island]] and the mainland. There, after a mutiny on an airship, a splinter renounced fire and embraced ice. They are on bad terms with the Cartel's centre. At the end of the Second Age (c. year 500) Svarog had them give up their igloos and build a real city on their island, the [[Frost Genasi City]].

## Now
They live in their city.
