**Created:** year zero (Second Age), by [[Astralis]] · **Orders:** [[Cradle]] · [[Star Chorus]] · [[Flaming Fist]] · [[Settlers]] · [[Mages' Guild]] · [[Mycomancers]] · [[Vengeful Paladins]]

## Story
Astralis concluded that the only way to multiply the faithful was to do the opposite of everything he had made so far. The humans named themselves, and were born neutral on the [[Great Plains]] along the [[River of Life]]. Their first order, the [[Cradle]], was one of pure survival and peace. Those who heard the [[Void Glance]] from afar became the [[Star Chorus]]. They built [[Greenhaven]], [[Heart of the Plains]] and [[Glaive Port]]. They joined the [[Centaurs]] in the [[Eternal Alliance]].

In the Third Age Glaive Port fell to the [[Orcs]], and its refugees bred the fanatical [[Vengeful Paladins]]. Humans and centaurs fight the [[Water Elves]] forever in the [[War Zone]]. Seeing humans become the dominant race, the [[Dwarves]] took Greenhaven.

## Now
They are the most numerous people and the dominant city-builders, found everywhere.
