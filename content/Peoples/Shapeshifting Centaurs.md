> Centaurs who, by ritual and magic, can take human form for a while.

**Created:** c. year 500 (Second Age), by [[Atlas]] · a sub-race of the [[Centaurs]] · **Order:** [[Order of the Eternal Peace]]

## Story
When [[Astralis]] bound [[Humans]] and centaurs in the [[Eternal Alliance]], [[Atlas]] gave the alliance a new, good sub-race of his own centaurs. He also founded among them the [[Order of the Eternal Peace]], sworn to keep the alliance from ever breaking, by any means necessary.

## Now
They live in the alliance's lands.

## Connections
- [[Eternal Alliance]]: their origin
- [[Order of the Eternal Peace]]: its sworn keepers
