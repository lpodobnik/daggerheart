# The Ice Giants

> Good healers out of the Frozen North, broken at Glaive Port and now rare.

**Created:** c. year 100 (Second Age), by [[Sylvanus]] · **Orders:** [[Hands of Elyndra]] · [[Purifiers of Evil]]

## Story
Sylvanus brought them out of the [[Frozen North]]. Their order, the [[Hands of Elyndra]], learned to channel healing magic through the crystal there. Around year 500 [[Elyndra]] readied them as a defensive army of ramparts and self-defence. Around year 600 she founded the [[Purifiers of Evil]] among them for a counter-offensive.

Around year 610 they laid siege to orc-held [[Glaive Port]]. Over six or seven years they froze the lake. Then they were **decisively defeated** ([[Elyndra's Defeat]]), and the battlefield became their [[Corpse Field]]. What remained withdrew to [[Coldspire]], with few healers left.

## Now
They are rare, withdrawn to Coldspire. Their crystal in the north is cracked.

## Connections
- [[Elyndra]]: their avatar-general
- [[Corpse Field]]: where their army died
- [[Coldspire]]: their last refuge
