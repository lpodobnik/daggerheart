**Created:** year zero (Second Age), by [[Atlas]] · **Orders:** [[Purebred]] · [[Time Keepers]]

## Story
They were made neutral nomads, with a small evil order of priests, the [[Purebred]], who hold that the union of beast and man makes them superior to every other race. [[Astralis]] made the women-led [[Time Keepers]] among them. The Time Keepers drew the first maps of the world and made the first calendars. Astralis also had them beat the [[Centaur Roads]] across the world (c. year 200). Around year 300 [[Sylvanus]] **purified** them, and they are now good. Around year 500 they joined the [[Humans]] in the [[Eternal Alliance]], which produced the [[Shapeshifting Centaurs]].

Around year 600 the [[Order of the Eternal Peace]] called humans and centaurs to arms in [[Greenhaven]]. [[Karros and Kassander]], a dwarf riding a centaur, led them into the [[War Zone]] against the [[Water Elves]].

## Now
They keep the roads and the alliance with humans, and fight in the War Zone.
