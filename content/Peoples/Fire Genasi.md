> Burning humanoids quickened from the glowing stones of Ardor's eruption. Evil, "like the Fire Nation".

**Created:** c. 3,000 years before year zero (First Age), by [[Svarog]] · **Orders:** [[Ardora's First]] · [[Cinderwing Cartel]] · [[Ember Dancers]]

## Story
[[Ardor]] erupted, and the chaos-energy of the cosmos quickened the glowing stones that fell. They too claim the name Children of Stone, and who came first is disputed with the [[Dwarves]]. Obsessed with bloodline, they keep [[Ardora's First]], the descendants of the very first, as their nobility. [[Astralis]] gave them the earth-organ: vents roaring where the volcano punctures the earth. "The first sound your race ever heard was the earth playing its organ."

[[Ardora]] founded [[Cinderforge]] for them (c. year 100). Living among geysers and steam vents, they discovered **engineering** and invented **steam power**. The [[Cinderwing Cartel]] built steam airships and a trade network, until the [[Skybreak]] grounded them. A mutiny on an airship split off the [[Frost Genasi]] (c. year 200). Around year 600 they raised an army on their island as the elven ships sailed past. Astralis gave them the [[Ember Dancers]] (c. year 620). Their crystal cracked in the [[Shattering]], and they ringed it with the Pillars of Magic Harnessing (c. year 640).

## Now
They use steam engineering and are harnessing their cracked crystal. The grounded Cartel chases [[Portals]] and meteor shards.
