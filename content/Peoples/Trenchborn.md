# The Trenchborn

> Strange beings from the sea: tentacles where the lower jaw should be, skin iridescent like the inside of a shell.

**Created:** c. 1,000 years before year zero (First Age), by [[Astralis]] · **Order:** [[Echoes of the Void]]

## Story
As Astralis walked his coloured shore ([[Coloured Sands]]), they came out of the sea. Where the lower half of a skull should be they have tentacles. They are few, and they reproduce by rituals not even they fully understand. "Trenchborn" is what other races call them. Their order, the [[Echoes of the Void]], senses that sound, dance and colour together can reach their ancestors, but does not yet know how. Trenchborn explorers mixing with dwarf explorers in [[Fungorn]] became the [[Deepkin]].

## Now
Nothing touched them in the Third Age. They remain a mystery.
