> Clicking weavers of vast silk webs whom no one has yet managed to speak with.

**Created:** c. year 500 (Second Age), by [[Astralis]], in the southern mountains · **Order:** [[Threadspinners]]

## Story
Some are centaur-like on a spider's body; some are humanoid with extra eyes. They are ranked by how truly spider they are. They click and build vast silk webs. Their order, the [[Threadspinners]] (*Svirači na nitima*), plays silk strings to speak and to pass on knowledge.

Around year 610 Astralis allied them with the [[Water Elves]] ([[Elf-Spider Alliance]]): a Switzerland, both neutral, trading sea-lore for the paths through the [[Misty Mountains]]. Some ten years later they helped the [[Water Druids]] drive Glaive Port's armies out of the [[Grove]] in the [[Great Battle of the Third Age]].

## Now
They are allied with the water elves, and helped save the Grove.
