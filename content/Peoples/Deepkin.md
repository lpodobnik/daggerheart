> Short and stout, with bearded tentacles: true mushroom-folk of Fungorn.

**Created:** c. year 100 (Second Age), by [[Astralis]] · born of [[Dwarves|dwarf]] and [[Trenchborn]] explorers · **Order:** [[Bent Eye]]

## Story
Astralis raised them in [[Fungorn]] from the mixing of dwarf explorers and Trenchborn explorers. Their shamans, the [[Bent Eye]], eat the mushrooms and build non-Euclidean structures no one has yet seen. The story goes that whoever sees one goes absolutely insane.

They breed by spores and are immune to the [[Fungal Plague]]. Around year 630 they raised an army: raising corpses with fungus is a travesty, and it was their forest that burned. Around year 640 they laid siege to [[Glaive Port]]. The city held, and the siege has no end ([[Siege of Glaive Port]]).

## Now
They are immune to the plague and besieging Glaive Port.

## Connections
- [[Mycomancers]]: the fungus-raisers they despise
