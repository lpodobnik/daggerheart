> Raised in the desert, living for glory and war.

**Created:** c. year 200 (Second Age), by [[Atlas]] · **Order:** [[Sandborn]]

## Story
Atlas raised them in the [[Obsidian Desert]] with an order of warriors, the [[Sandborn]]. They became **masters of war beasts** (c. year 300). Atlas raised them armies, founded their [[Orc War Camps]] (c. year 400), and the [[Hollow Knight]] gave them a second army (c. year 500).

Around year 600 the Hollow Knight led them to take [[Glaive Port]] ([[Fall of Glaive Port]]), the heart of a [[Great Orkish Empire]] in the making. But the occupiers found city life better than the desert, and their ruling caste rotted into brutes. The desert orcs said *that is not the way*. They gathered the magic crystals of the sands and fused them into the obelisk of the [[Final End]]. Around year 610 the Hollow Knight, disgusted, abandoned them.

## Now
The desert orcs are good but fanatical and keep the Final End; their camps have no armies left. The Glaive Port orcs are corrupted and are nominal rulers only, beneath the [[Mycomancers]].
