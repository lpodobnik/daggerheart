> Wholly tribal and evil, ruled by nobles who killed every sibling in their clutch.

**Created:** c. year 100 (Second Age), by [[Atlas]], in [[Sylvanus]]'s jungles · **Orders:** [[Atlas's First]] · [[Arcane Fang]]

## Story
Atlas made them on [[Jungle Island]]. Their nobles, [[Atlas's First]], each earned the rank by killing every sibling from his own clutch of eggs. [[Sylvariel]] slipped among them and founded the [[Arcane Fang]], who want every crystal for their sons. Sylvanus gave them a "city" (c. year 200): a chain of jungle villages, a civilisation of the Amazon.

Around year 630 they took up arms. The [[Hollow Knight]] raised a second army of them. Sylvanus sent them through the [[Underdark]] against the [[Tree of Life]], but the [[Grove]]'s misdirection held, its assassins killed their chiefs, and they turned on each other. The Hollow Knight built them a second war camp, and they split into two warring factions around the Underdark mouth. [[Elyndra]] and the [[Water Elves]] took that mouth around year 640. The lizardmen were broken but not destroyed.

## Now
They are in civil war: two factions. Their war camps on [[Jungle Island]] still stand, and the elves hold the island's Underdark mouth. The remnants of the two armies that went through the Underdark camp at the mainland mouth, fighting each other.
