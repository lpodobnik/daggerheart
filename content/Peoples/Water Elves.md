> Skilled sailors who spread through the islands along the cold currents. Once neutral, now decadent.

**Created:** c. 2,000 years before year zero (First Age), by [[Sylvanus]] · **Orders:** [[Explorers]] · [[Last Tide]] · [[Astral Chord]] · [[Elf Outpost Marines]] · [[Water Druids]]

## Story
They were made skilled and neutral, and the bamboo let them build rafts to spread through [[Sylmara Archipelago|Sylmara]]. The [[Explorers]] set out to find what lies on far shores. One party of them, turned by [[Sylvariel]], became the xenophobic [[Last Tide]] of [[Nerathis]]. The [[Astral Chord]] learned bamboo music from the stars. Their cities multiplied: [[Selvaruun]], the [[Elf Outpost]], and the [[Twin City]].

In the Third Age the Last Tide raided the northern shores and bound itself to [[Glaive Port]] ([[Evil Alliance]]). The elves hid the [[Grove]] around the [[Tree of Life]], allied with the [[Spider People]] ([[Elf-Spider Alliance]]), and saw off Glaive Port's armies in the [[Great Battle of the Third Age]]. [[Karros and Kassander]] fought them to a standstill in the [[War Zone]]. Their island's crystal was the one that **shattered**. Sylvariel led them against the [[Lizardmen]], and with [[Elyndra]] they took [[Jungle Island]]'s Underdark mouth. Then, having always had it easy, they grew arrogant and decadent, and the whole civilisation was corrupted (c. year 640, [[Svarog]]).

## Now
They hold a foothold on the north shores and one Underdark mouth on Jungle Island. They fight the endless War Zone. The Last Tide's navy cannot build new ships for a generation.
