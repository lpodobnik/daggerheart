> Six great crystals fuel all magic, one per school. Four are known, and two are lost.

**Appeared:** c. year zero (Second Age), by [[Sylvanus]] ([[Coming of Magic]]) · marked on the map as four red ovals

## The six
| Crystal | State |
|---|---|
| [[Elaruun]]: the dragon's island, behind the winds | cracked |
| The [[Last Tide]]'s island ([[Nerathis]]) | **shattered** |
| The [[Frozen North]], near [[Coldspire]] | cracked |
| [[Cinderforge]] | cracked, ringed by the Pillars of Magic Harnessing |
| Lost | unknown; one of the two lost crystals **governs portals** |
| Lost | unknown |

## Story
Floating crystals appeared in the air in four places, and in time the races learned that these fuel magic. The [[Dwarves]] melded their magic with [[Star Metal]]. The [[Hands of Elyndra]] learned healing through the northern crystal. The [[Arcane Fang]] want them all. Around year 500 the gods admitted there are **six**, and that where the other two lie no one knows, not even the gods. [[Svarog]] prophesied to the [[Cinderwing Cartel]] that one of them governs [[Portals]].

Around year 630 [[Atlas]] tried to break them all ([[Shattering]]). One shattered, chosen, it is said, by chance, and the rest cracked. [[Ardora]] had Cinderforge ring its crystal with tall coils that catch the leaking magic and feed it to the city.

## Now
The cracks are spreading slowly, like sand through an hourglass. One day there may be no magic left. Magic grows wilder the nearer you come to the broken crystal, and the surge made the [[Monstrosities]]. The [[Lost City]] may know where the lost two lie.
