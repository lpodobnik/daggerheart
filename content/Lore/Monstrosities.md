> The world had no ogres before. Now it does.

**Origin:** the [[Shattering]], c. year 630 (Third Age), by [[Atlas]]

## Story
When Atlas broke one of the [[Great Crystals]] and cracked the rest, the surge ran across the whole continent. Plants, beasts and everything living twisted into monstrosities.

## Now
They are everywhere. Their density rises toward the [[Last Tide]]'s island ([[Nerathis]]), where the crystal shattered and magic is wildest.

## Connections
- [[Shattering]]: their cause
- [[Great Crystals]]: the broken source
