> The dwarves cannot see it, but they *feel* it glinting in the fissures.

**Seeded:** c. 3,000 years before year zero (First Age), by [[Astralis]], into the rock of [[Sky Spear]] (and near the volcano)

## Story
Star metal is one reason the [[Dwarves]] never left the place they were born. They built [[Kharag Duun]] above the deposit c. 2,000 years before year zero, taking it as a sign that the powers had made a home for them. Around year 100 they left the Bronze Age by melding magic from the [[Great Crystals]] with star metal, and became smiths whose creations blend the two. The [[Stone Talus]] raised the [[Magical Forge]] in the same years. For five centuries the dwarves did little but sell star metal at the [[Bazaar]].

## Now
Kharag Duun is full of diplomats and star metal.
