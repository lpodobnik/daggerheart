## The Ages
| Age | Name | Lasted | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | The Shaping | c. 6,000 years | c. 6,000 years before year zero → year zero |
| II | The Coming of the Races | c. 600 years | year zero → c. year 600 |
| III | The Age of War | about fifty years | c. year 600 → c. year 650 |

*Every Age begins and ends with the fall of a meteor of unknown origin* ([[Meteors]]). The Third Age was an age of armies.

- [[First Meteor]]: c. 6,000 years before year zero, the start of the First Age
- [[Second Meteor]]: **year zero**, the start of the Second Age
- [[Third Meteor]]: c. year 650, the end of the Third Age

## Year zero
The [[Time Keepers]] date all time from the [[Second Meteor]], which blew open the [[First Fortress]] at the start of the Second Age. The [[Third Meteor]] fell c. year 650 and ended the Third Age.

## Calendars
Around year 100 [[Astralis]] made the Time Keepers among the [[Centaurs]], a women-led order. They found that by watching the sky they could align their cycles and rituals. Watching the night sky, they drew the first maps of the world and made the first **calendars**.

## No moon
There is no moon yet, and so no tides.
