> Mindless and leaderless, in every stage of decay.

**Rose:** c. year 630 (Third Age), by [[Astralis]]

## Story
The dead of the [[Great Battle of the Third Age]] rose as the [[Fungal Plague]] made them into zombies: evil, and existing without purpose. In the same years [[Malakai]] founded the [[Mycomancers]], who command the fungal dead. To the [[Deepkin]], raising corpses with fungus is a travesty.

## Now
The dead patrol [[Glaive Port]]'s streets without harming anyone. More rise in the [[Wasteland]] from the battlefield.

## Connections
- [[Mycomancers]]: their masters
- [[Fungal Plague]]: their cause
- [[Wasteland]]: where they rise
