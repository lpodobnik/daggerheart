> An empire in the making, with Glaive Port as its heart, and it rotted before it was born.

**Begun:** c. year 600 (Third Age), by [[Atlas]], at the [[Fall of Glaive Port]]

## Story
When the [[Hollow Knight]] took [[Glaive Port]] with two [[Orcs|orc]] armies, the city became the heart of a Great Orkish Empire in the making. But the occupiers found city life better than the desert, and their ruling caste rotted into brutes. The desert orcs rejected that way and turned to the [[Final End]]. Some ten years later the Hollow Knight abandoned the orcs. Glaive Port's four armies later died or scattered in the [[Great Battle of the Third Age]].

## Now
Orcs are only the nominal rulers of Glaive Port. The [[Mycomancers]] rule in fact.
