> Small, obsidian-like crystals born of hot sand and dry air, which amplify magic.

**Appeared:** c. year 500 (Second Age), by [[Sylvanus]], in the south-eastern desert ([[Obsidian Desert]])

## Story
Sylvanus made the south-eastern desert grow them. The first mines opened, and they became very valuable. [[Glaive Port]] was the nearest walled city to the new mines and to the [[Orcs]], and it raised an army. Around year 600 the desert orcs gathered the magic crystals of the sands and fused them into a great obelisk in their central war camp: the [[Final End]].

## Now
They are mined. The Final End amplifies magic, and no one understands it.
