> *Every Age begins and ends with the fall of a meteor of unknown origin.*

## The three
| Meteor | When | Where | What it did |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[First Meteor]] | c. 6,000 years before year zero (First Age) | [[World's Breach]] | The round crater; its shards enable [[Portals]] |
| [[Second Meteor]] | year zero (Second Age) | [[First Fortress]] | Blew it open on the migrating [[Dwarves]]; **year zero** |
| [[Third Meteor]] | c. year 650 (end of the Third Age) | the mountains by [[Heart of the Plains]] | Quake and mudslides; the [[Ballista Tower]] cracked; [[Sky Spear]] porous |

All three were [[Svarog]]'s. The last fell where chaos chose.

## Traces
- Shards of the first make portal magic possible nearby, and the [[Cinderwing Cartel]] knows it.
- The [[Marrow Munchers]] smear slime from the fallen star on themselves as war paint.
- The [[Mages' Guild]] buys up meteor fragments.
- The [[Time Keepers]] date time from the second ([[Ages and Time]]).

## Now
The third just fell. The second meteor's site has never been investigated.
