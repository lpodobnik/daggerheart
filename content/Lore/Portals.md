> Possible only near shards of the first meteor. Nearly impossible elsewhere.

## Story
Around year 300 [[Astralis]] opened a ley-line portal: the [[Void Glance]], a brief glimpse into the void. Some hundred years later, around year 500, [[Svarog]] sent the grounded [[Cinderwing Cartel]] a prophecy: one of the two lost [[Great Crystals]] governs portals, and they were to learn everything of portals, other dimensions and time. Then he made them masters of it. They found that **shards of the [[First Meteor]]** (the one that made [[World's Breach]]) make portal magic far stronger nearby, and that far from the shards it is almost impossible.

## Now
The Cartel has mastered portals and chases portal secrets and meteor shards. The [[Second Meteor]]'s site, the [[First Fortress]], has not been investigated. The [[Mages' Guild]] buys meteor fragments.
