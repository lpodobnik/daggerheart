**Avatar of [[Atlas]]** · appeared c. 3,000 years before year zero, in the First Age

## Story
The Talus rose between the hills and the mountains. Atlas gathered lightning at the spear-tip of [[Sky Spear]] and struck him, and the pieces that fell became proto-[[Dwarves]]. The dwarves worship the Talus and the one who made him. At Atlas's command:
- **c. 2,000 years before year zero** the dwarves built [[Kharag Duun]] above the [[Star Metal]] deposit
- **c. year 100** he raised the [[Magical Forge]], fed with magma from the mountain's depths
- **c. year 500** he founded the [[Bazaar]], a city with no walls where the roads cross the river

Around year 600 he was Atlas's hand when the [[Order of the Eternal Peace]] called humans and centaurs to arms in [[Greenhaven]]. Around year 630 he raised another dwarf army, and some ten years later he marched with the dwarves and took Greenhaven as its wardens.

## Now
He led the dwarves into Greenhaven, which is now dwarf-ruled and governed by the [[Magisters]].
