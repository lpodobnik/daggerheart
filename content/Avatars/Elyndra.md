> "The Angel of Vengeance". She was beaten at Glaive Port and won at the Underdark mouth.

**Avatar of [[Sylvanus]]** · appeared c. year 400 (Second Age) · female, good

## Story
The [[Ice Giants]]' healing order, the [[Hands of Elyndra]], bears her name. Around year 500 she readied the giants as a defensive army of ramparts and self-defence. Around year 600 she founded the [[Purifiers of Evil]] for a counter-offensive against orc-held [[Glaive Port]]. Around year 610 she led the Healers and the Purifiers to liberate the city and was **decisively defeated** ([[Elyndra's Defeat]]). Her army was destroyed and she escaped, and the battlefield became the [[Corpse Field]].

Around year 620, wounded, she built a city on the coast below the forest of peace ([[Elyndra's Coastal City]]). Around year 640 she gathered an army against the [[Lizardmen]]. Together with the [[Water Elves]] she took [[Jungle Island]]'s [[Underdark]] mouth. The lizardmen were broken but not destroyed.

## Now
She is available for consultation.
