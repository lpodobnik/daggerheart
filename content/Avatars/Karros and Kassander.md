> A good dwarf, Karros, riding the centaur Kassander.

**Avatar of [[Astralis]]** · appeared c. year 400 (Second Age), in the centre of the continent

## Story
They appeared in the centre of the continent and began to act.
- **c. year 600** raised an army in [[Heart of the Plains]]
- **c. year 630** led the armies of Heart of the Plains and [[Greenhaven]] (the "Liberatorians") against the [[Water Elves]] on their land. It ended even and became the [[War Zone]], where humans and centaurs fight elves without end.
- **c. year 640** came home from the front, found the [[Dwarves]] ruling Greenhaven, and turned them to the honourable way

## Now
Fighting in the south.

## Connections
- [[Greenhaven]]: the city they purified, now contested with the [[Magisters]]
- [[War Zone]]: their endless stalemate
- [[Heart of the Plains]]: raised its army
