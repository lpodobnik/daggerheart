> Corrupted Glaive Port bound to the Last Tide: the city, not the orcish race.

**c. year 600 (Third Age), by [[Sylvanus]]**

## Story
After the [[Fall of Glaive Port]] and its corruption, Sylvanus bound the city to the [[Last Tide]], the secluded, xenophobic elves whose warships raided the northern shores in those same years. The pact binds the city of [[Glaive Port]], not the [[Orcs]] as a race.
