# The Elf-Spider Alliance

> A Switzerland, both neutral: sea-lore traded for the paths through the Misty Mountains.

**c. year 610 (Third Age), by [[Astralis]]**

## Story
Astralis allied the [[Water Elves]] and the [[Spider People]]. The elves trade sea-lore, and the Spider People trade the paths through the [[Misty Mountains]].

## Consequences
- Around year 620 the Spider People helped the [[Water Druids]] drive Glaive Port's armies out of the [[Grove]] ([[Great Battle of the Third Age]]).

## Connections
- [[Spider People]]: one party
- [[Water Elves]]: the other
