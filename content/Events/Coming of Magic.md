> In four places, floating crystals appeared in the air. Magic had entered the world.

**c. year zero (Second Age), by [[Sylvanus]]**

## Story
Sylvanus changed the world. In four places the [[Great Crystals]] appeared, floating in the air, and in time the races learned that they are what fuels magic. The four known crystals lie on [[Elaruun]], on the [[Last Tide]]'s island ([[Nerathis]]), in the [[Frozen North]] near [[Coldspire]], and at [[Cinderforge]].

## Consequences
- **c. year 100** the [[Dwarves]] meld crystal magic with [[Star Metal]]. The [[Hands of Elyndra]] learn healing through the northern crystal. The [[Arcane Fang]] want every crystal for their sons.
- **c. year 500** there are **six**, not four. Two are lost, and one of those governs [[Portals]].
- **c. year 630** the [[Shattering]] breaks one and cracks the rest.
