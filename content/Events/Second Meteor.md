> "The falling star that landed far in the west with a great roar." Year zero.

**Year zero (Second Age), by [[Svarog]]** · a smaller meteor, some two megatons

## Story
The Second Age began as the First did, with a stone from the sky, but this time it was catastrophe. Svarog dropped it on the [[First Fortress]] at the very moment the [[Dwarves]] were migrating into it, and blew the Fortress open with terrible loss. An entire dwarven order that had split off to go ahead was wiped out.

## Consequences
- The dwarves remember it as their great wound, the one that only hardened them.
- The [[Time Keepers]] date all time from it: **year zero** ([[Ages and Time]]).
- The site has never been investigated.
