# The Collapse of the Left Eye

> Astralis, angry that Atlas would suppress magic, struck him in the eye.

**c. year 640 (Third Age), by [[Astralis]]**

## Story
The [[Left Eye of Atlas]] is a near-perfect ring of mountain holding a deep lake at enormous height. It collapsed on one side, and its waters flooded the [[War Zone]].

## Consequences
- The War Zone became a swamp.
- The armies there stopped knowing friend from foe. Men who had been killing each other hauled each other out of the quicksand.

## Connections
- [[Shattering]]: the cause
- [[War Zone]]: the swamp it made
- [[Atlas]]: the god struck
