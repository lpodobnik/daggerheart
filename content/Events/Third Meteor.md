> The Age ended as it began, with a meteor.

**c. year 650 (end of the Third Age), by [[Svarog]]**

## Story
Svarog, god of chaos, let it fall where chaos chose. It struck the mountain slopes beside the Ballista's city, [[Heart of the Plains]], and tore the glaciers off the highest peaks on the continent.

## Consequences
- An earthquake and mudslides broke the city and buried armies. They also made another swamp and a crater-lake.
- The [[Ballista Tower]] cracked and is out of action.
- [[Sky Spear]]'s ancient ice split and drained, and the mountain is now porous. Tunnels and caverns are open where none were, and who knows what lives in them.
- It ended the Third Age. The campaign opens a few months to a year later, c. year 650–651.
