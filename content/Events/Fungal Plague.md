> Out of the burned mushroom forest spread a plague: sterility, and death if untreated.

**c. year 620 (Third Age), by [[Svarog]]**

## Story
It spread from the burned part of [[Fungorn]]. The acute phase brings sterility, and untreated it brings death. It passes by blood and saliva. It was worst in [[Glaive Port]], then the [[Bazaar]], then the island. The mushroom-folk ([[Deepkin]]), who breed by spores, are immune. Scholars think it is the fungi's own defence.

## Consequences
- Famine came to Glaive Port as the fungus took livestock, fish and crops. It raised a fourth army and marched south ([[Great Battle of the Third Age]]).
- The dead of that battlefield rose as [[Fungal Zombies]] (c. year 630).
- [[Malakai]], a leper who healed himself, founded the [[Mycomancers]], who command the fungal dead.

## Now
It is active. The plague has already been through the Bazaar.
