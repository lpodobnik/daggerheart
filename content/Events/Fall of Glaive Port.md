> No massacre, no pillage: curfew, martial law, and a trading city still trading.

**c. year 600 (Third Age), by [[Atlas]]** · the first great battle of the Age

## Story
The [[Hollow Knight]] (the Doombringer) led two [[Orcs|orc]] armies and their war beasts against [[Glaive Port]], and took it. Nothing was destroyed. The city's defenders went partisan.

## Consequences
- Glaive Port became the heart of a [[Great Orkish Empire]] in the making.
- [[Elyndra]] founded the [[Purifiers of Evil]] for a counter-offensive.
- The occupiers found city life better than the desert, and their ruling caste rotted into brutes ([[Astralis]] corrupted the city).
- The desert orcs said *that is not the way* and built the [[Final End]].
- [[Sylvanus]] bound the corrupted city to the [[Last Tide]] in the [[Evil Alliance]].
- Its refugees later bred the [[Vengeful Paladins]].
