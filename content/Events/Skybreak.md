> Magical storms close the sky. Nothing may fly.

**c. year 200 (Second Age), by [[Sylvanus]]**

## Story
Sylvanus closed the sky with magical storms that prevent all flying travel. The knowledge of flight is not lost, but nothing may fly.

## Consequences
- The [[Cinderwing Cartel]]'s steam airships are grounded.
- With the sky closed and the [[Centaur Roads]] crowded, [[Atlas]] made the [[Halflings]] (c. year 500).

## Now
The sky is still closed.

## Connections
- [[Cinderwing Cartel]]: grounded by it
- [[Halflings]]: born to fill the gap
