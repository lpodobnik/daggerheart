# Elyndra's Defeat

> The Ice Angel came to liberate Glaive Port and was decisively defeated.

**c. year 610 (Third Age), by [[Sylvanus]]**

## Story
[[Elyndra]] led the Healers ([[Hands of Elyndra]]) and the [[Purifiers of Evil]] to liberate orc-held [[Glaive Port]]. The siege lasted six or seven years. The giants froze the lake, and fishing and trade died. Then her army was destroyed, and she escaped.

## Consequences
- The battlefield became the [[Corpse Field]] of the [[Ice Giants]].
- The pursuers burned part of the mushroom forest ([[Fungorn]]). The river was rerouted, and the lake began to dry.
- Starving Glaive Port fell to **cannibalism**: [[Svarog]]'s avatar corrupted it again, and it is evil now.
- The remnant of the Ice Giants later withdrew to [[Coldspire]].
- The [[Fungal Plague]] spread out of the burned forest (c. year 620).
