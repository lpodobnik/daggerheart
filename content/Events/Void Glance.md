> A brief glimpse into the void, felt across the whole world.

**c. year 300 (Second Age), by [[Astralis]]**

## Story
Astralis opened a ley-line portal. Its epicentre was near the [[Twins]], and everyone close to it was marked by how they met it:
- **Within the crater's radius:** they simply ceased, taken into a cosmic horror that left nothing to bury.
- **Watched it with their own eyes:** they lived, but carry a sickness ever after, *the Void Glance*. They see everything in broken, multidimensional angles no one else can follow.
- **Heard it from far off:** among them a band of [[Humans]] found patterns in the sound and set them down as dissonant, terrible hymns. They became the [[Star Chorus]].

The [[Goblins]], whose mountains lie nearby, were simply among those caught.

## Consequences
- The epicentre is a crater in the shape of a five-pointed star, as [[Star Lake]] is.
- [[World's Breach]], far to the west, was untouched.
