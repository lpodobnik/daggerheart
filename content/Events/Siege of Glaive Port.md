# The Siege of Glaive Port

> The Deepkin laid siege to Glaive Port, and it held: a siege without end.

**c. year 640 (Third Age)** · [[Astralis]] (the besiegers) against [[Svarog]] (the city)

## Story
The [[Deepkin]] had raised an army some ten years before, around year 630. Raising corpses with fungus is a travesty to them, and it was their forest that burned. They marched on [[Glaive Port]], where [[Melakai]] had just raised a defensive army. The two sides were perfectly matched, and the siege became endless.

## Now
The siege is endless. The [[Mycomancers]] rule inside and the fungal dead patrol the streets.
