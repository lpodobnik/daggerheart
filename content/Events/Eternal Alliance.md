> Humans and centaurs, bound for shared prosperity and defence.

**c. year 500 (Second Age), by [[Astralis]]**, with [[Atlas]]'s order to keep it

## Story
Astralis bound [[Humans]] and [[Centaurs]] in an alliance signed in the central city ([[Greenhaven]]).

## Consequences
- A new, good sub-race came of it: the [[Shapeshifting Centaurs]].
- Atlas founded among them the [[Order of the Eternal Peace]], sworn to keep the alliance from ever breaking, by any means necessary.
- Around year 600 the Order called humans and centaurs to arms in Greenhaven.
- Humans and centaurs fight side by side in the [[War Zone]].

## Connections
- [[Shapeshifting Centaurs]]: born of it
- [[Order of the Eternal Peace]]: its keepers
