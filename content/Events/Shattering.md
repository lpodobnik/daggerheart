# The Shattering

> Atlas poured all his power into breaking magic itself.

**c. year 630 (Third Age), by [[Atlas]]**

## Story
Atlas was disgusted that war had become druids and misdirection instead of steel on steel, and he tried to shatter the [[Great Crystals]]. Only **one** shattered: the one on the [[Last Tide]]'s island, chosen, it is said, by chance. **The rest cracked.**

## Consequences
- The cracks are still spreading, slowly, like sand through an hourglass. One day there may be no magic left.
- The surge ran across the whole continent, and plants, beasts and everything living twisted into [[Monstrosities]]. The world had no ogres before; now it does.
- Magic survives, but the nearer the broken crystal, the wilder it gets.
- [[Svarog]], delighted and baffled, founded the [[Mages' Guild]]. [[Astralis]], angry, collapsed the [[Left Eye of Atlas]] ([[Collapse of the Left Eye]]).
- [[Cinderforge]] ringed its cracked crystal with the Pillars of Magic Harnessing.

## Now
Mortals don't know that a god, Atlas, did it on purpose.
