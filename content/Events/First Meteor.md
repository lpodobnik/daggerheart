> Svarog let chaos choose his place, and struck the near-centre of the continent with something from the sky.

**c. 6,000 years before year zero (First Age), by [[Svarog]]**

## Story
The impact left a crater as deep as a canyon and perfectly round, riddled with tunnels and cavities. It was perhaps once far deeper than anyone now knows, and the races would one day call it [[World's Breach]]. Around it Svarog baked the land into a desert where no moisture survives. Some thousand years later, c. 5,000 years before year zero, [[Atlas]]'s sister river came to pour over its rim in the Breach Falls.

## Consequences
- **Shards of the first meteor** make portal magic far stronger nearby. Far from the shards it is almost impossible. The [[Cinderwing Cartel]] learned this around year 500 ([[Portals]]).
- Nobody knows what the meteor was.
