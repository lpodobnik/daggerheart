> Glaive Port's four armies marched south for bread, and the forest swallowed them.

**c. year 620 (Third Age)** · [[Svarog]] (Glaive Port) against [[Sylvanus]] (the Grove) · the second great battle of the Age

## Story
Famine-struck [[Glaive Port]] sent its four armies south against the [[Grove]]. The [[Water Druids]] confused them. Their generals vanished, eaten by fungus or by trees. With the [[Spider People]], the druids drove them out.

## Consequences
- Fewer than half died. The rest scattered as bandits and marauders across a devastated [[Wasteland]] east of the Grove, along the road into the desert.
- The Grove survived, wounded.
- The dead of the battlefield rose as [[Fungal Zombies]] (c. year 630).
