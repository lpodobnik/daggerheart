> Where the Ice Giants came to free Glaive Port, and died.

**Made:** c. year 610 (Third Age) — [[Elyndra's Defeat]] (by [[Sylvanus]]).

## Story
[[Elyndra]] led the Healers — the [[Hands of Elyndra]] — and the [[Purifiers of Evil]] to liberate orc-held [[Glaive Port]], and was **decisively defeated**. Her army was destroyed and she escaped. The siege had lasted six or seven years; the [[Ice Giants]] had frozen the lake, and fishing and trade died. Her pursuers burned part of the mushroom forest, [[Fungorn]]; the river was rerouted and the lake began to dry. The battlefield became the Corpse Field of the Ice Giants.

Some ten years later, around year 620, what remained of the giants withdrew to [[Coldspire]], rare now, with few healers left.
