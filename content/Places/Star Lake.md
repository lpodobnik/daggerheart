> A lake shaped like a five-pointed star, under a sky nothing can cloud.

**Made:** c. 6,000 years before year zero (First Age), by [[Astralis]].

## Story
[[Astralis]] flooded a great region with lakes and at their heart set one shaped like a five-pointed star.

Around 1,000 years before year zero a shape rose out of Star Lake — humanoid, though there were no humanoids yet, silver, with no face and no sex: the [[Silver One]], Astralis's avatar. In the same years Astralis fixed the sky above the lake: always clear; nothing can cloud it, only wind.

The [[Void Glance]] crater of Age II bears the lake's five-pointed shape.

## Now
Nothing recorded since Age I. The sky above it is still clear.
