> Where the Frost Genasi gave up their igloos for a real city, on the island amid the ice floes.

**Built:** c. year 500 (Second Age), by [[Svarog]]

## Story
Around year 200, a [[Cinderwing Cartel]] airship trading far and wide came to the island amid the ice floes bridging [[Jungle Island]] and the mainland. After a mutiny its crew renounced fire, embraced ice and lost their cinder to the cold — the [[Frost Genasi]], on bad terms with the Cartel's centre.

Around year 500, late in the Second Age, [[Svarog]] had the Frost Genasi give up their igloos and build a real city on their island.

## Connections
