# Selvaruun

> The City of Scholars, on the dragon's island behind the winds.

**Founded:** c. year 400 (Second Age), by [[Sylvanus]] · **Where:** [[Elaruun]]

## Story
[[Sylvanus]] founded Selvaruun, the City of Scholars, on [[Elaruun]] — the western island of the [[Sylmara Archipelago]], where [[Sylvariel]] the Dragon lives and one of the four known [[Great Crystals|great crystals]] lies. The archipelago's permanent winds drive any sailor away, so the islands are almost impossible to reach.

## Now
The City of Scholars. Since the [[Shattering]], every great crystal but the broken one is cracked — Elaruun's among them.
