# The Lost City of Knowledge and History

> An empty city somewhere in the former desert, whose keepers wrote down the whole history of the world.

**Hidden:** c. year 640 (Third Age), by [[Sylvanus]]

## Story
Around year 640, late in the Age of War, [[Sylvanus]] hid a Lost City of Knowledge and History somewhere in the former desert: empty, dangerous, perhaps haunted, perhaps guarded by old machines. Its keepers recorded the whole unabridged history of the world, and it may know where the two lost [[Great Crystals|great crystals]] are — one of which governs [[Portals|portals]].

## Now
No one in the world knows where it is. Its existence is a secret the gods keep.
