# The Frozen North

> The far end of the world: hilly, bitterly cold — snow, ice, the pole.

**Made:** c. 1,000 years before year zero (First Age), by [[Sylvanus]].

## Story
[[Sylvanus]] made the far end of the world hilly and bitterly cold. The [[Silver One]], Astralis's avatar, wandered there and was snowed on.

At the [[Coming of Magic]] (year zero) one of the four known [[Great Crystals]] appeared here, near where Coldspire would stand. Around year 100 Sylvanus brought the [[Ice Giants]] out of the Frozen North — good healers — whose order, the [[Hands of Elyndra]], learned through the crystal to channel healing magic. Around year 300 [[Atlas]] founded [[Coldspire]] in the cold north, stone and ice on a raised hill.

The giants went to liberate [[Glaive Port]] and were broken ([[Elyndra's Defeat]], c. year 610); some ten years later, around year 620, what remained withdrew to Coldspire. At the [[Shattering]] (c. year 630) the northern crystal cracked.

## Now
The crystal is cracked; the last Ice Giants, rare now and with few healers left, shelter in Coldspire.
