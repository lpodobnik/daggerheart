> Not merely black sand, but sand of ground obsidian — volcanic glass.

**Made:** c. 2,000 years before year zero (First Age), by [[Atlas]], joined to [[Svarog]]'s arid crater-land.

## Story
[[Atlas]] spread a great desert across the south-east; with Svarog's arid land around [[World's Breach]] it became the Obsidian Desert. In the same years Svarog raised the [[Black Mesa]] above it.

Around year 200 an empty suit of obsidian plate stood up out of its sands — the Doombringer, later the [[Hollow Knight]] — and Atlas raised the [[Orcs]] here, living for glory and war, with their warrior order, the [[Sandborn]]. Around year 300 Atlas raised an army in the south-eastern desert, and around year 400 founded the orcs' [[Orc War Camps|War Camps]]. Around year 500, late in Age II, [[Sylvanus]] made the south-eastern desert grow [[Desert Crystals|crystals]] — small, obsidian-like, born of hot sand and dry air, amplifying magic; the first mines opened, and [[Glaive Port]] became the nearest walled city to them. The Doombringer raised the orcs a second army.

Around year 600 the Hollow Knight led both armies against Glaive Port. The orcs who stayed said *that is not the way*: they gathered the crystals of the sands and fused them into a great obelisk in their central war camp — the [[Final End]].

## Now
The desert orcs, good but fanatical, keep the Final End; the war camps have no armies left. The crystals are still mined.
