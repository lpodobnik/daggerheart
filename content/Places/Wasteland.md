# The Wasteland

> East of the Grove, along the road into the desert — a devastated land of scattered soldiers turned bandit — and dead who will not stay down.

**Made:** c. year 620 (Third Age) — the [[Great Battle of the Third Age]] ([[Svarog]]'s armies against [[Sylvanus]]'s Grove).

## Story
Famine came to [[Glaive Port]] as the [[Fungal Plague]] took livestock, fish and crops, and its four armies marched south for bread, against the [[Grove]]. The [[Water Druids]] confused them, their generals vanished — eaten by fungus or by trees — and with the [[Spider People]] the Grove drove them out; the forest swallowed them. Fewer than half died. The rest scattered as bandits and marauders across a devastated wasteland. It is called the graveyard of the fallen armies.

Some ten years later, around year 630, the dead of the battlefield rose: the plague made them [[Fungal Zombies]], mindless and leaderless, in every stage of decay ([[Astralis]]).

## Now
Glaive Port's scattered armies turned bandit, and fungal zombies rising from the battlefield.
