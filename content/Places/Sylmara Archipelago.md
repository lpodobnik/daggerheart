> The first land of the world: islands in the western sea, ringed by winds that turn every sailor back.

**Raised:** c. 6,000 years before year zero (First Age), by [[Sylvanus]] — the first act of creation.

## Story
[[Sylvanus]] raised an archipelago in the western sea, shaped it, and set permanent winds about it that drive any sailor away, so that the islands are almost impossible to reach. At the same time [[Astralis]] grew a bamboo here like no other — waterproof yet hard and supple — with as yet no one to use it.

Its islands include [[Elaruun]], the dragon's island; and [[Nyravel]], the Last Tide's island, with its city [[Nerathis]]. Around 2,000 years before year zero the [[Water Elves]] were made and spread through the islands along the cold currents, the bamboo letting them build rafts; their [[Explorers]] set out for far shores, and a party that turned inward became the [[Last Tide]]. Around year 100 the [[Astral Chord]] learned that the bamboo, bent and poured just so, gives tones that stun any listener.

With the [[Coming of Magic]] two of the four known [[Great Crystals]] stood here — on [[Elaruun]] and on the Last Tide's island. At the [[Shattering]] (c. year 630) the Last Tide's crystal broke, chosen by chance, and the rest cracked.

## Now
The Last Tide's island is wild magic and [[Monstrosities]], its decadent navy unable to build new ships for a generation. [[Sylvariel]] keeps to [[Elaruun]]. The winds still hold.
