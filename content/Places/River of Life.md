> The river a god of war drew when his heart softened.

**Made:** c. 2,000 years before year zero (First Age), by [[Atlas]].

## Story
Watching what the [[Stone Talus]] had made and what [[Astralis]] had made, [[Atlas]] felt his heart of conflict soften, and drew a river to feed the [[Great Plains]]: the River of Life.

In the first years after year zero the [[Humans]] were born on the Great Plains along the River of Life. Around year 300 [[Sylvanus]] founded [[Greenhaven]] on its banks, the central city — later the home of writing, the Library, and the [[Eternal Alliance]].

## Now
[[Greenhaven]] on its banks is dwarf-ruled, torn between the honourable way and the [[Magisters]].
