# The Obsidian Spires

> A coast that looks unnatural: a forest of glass spires that glows day and night.

**Made:** c. 5,000 years before year zero (First Age), by [[Astralis]].

## Story
[[Astralis]] planted a forest of obsidian spires some fifteen metres tall along a coast. They make the shore unapproachable and reflect light, day and night, casting an uncanny glow. In the same years [[Sylvanus]] added a huge lake and a river running to a second delta near the unnatural coast.

## Connections
- [[Astralis]] — its maker
- [[Sylvanus]] — the lake and delta beside it
