# The Twins

> Two enormous cliffs with a gap between, and an inland sea between the peaks.

**Raised:** c. 1,000 years before year zero (First Age), by [[Atlas]], in the south-west.

## Story
[[Atlas]] raised two enormous cliffs with a gap between them, and the sea ran in to fill an inland sea between the two peaks.

Around year 300 [[Astralis]] opened a ley-line portal — a brief glimpse into the void, felt across the whole world — and its epicentre was near the Twins. Those within the crater's radius simply ceased; those who watched it lived with the sickness the world named the [[Void Glance]]; those who only heard it included the humans who became the [[Star Chorus]]. The [[Goblins]], whose mountains lie nearby, were among those caught. The epicentre was left a crater in the shape of a **five-pointed star**, as [[Star Lake]] is.

## Now
The Void Glance crater lies near.
