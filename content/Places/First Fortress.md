> A natural fortress the size of a country, walls of almost perfectly flat stone — broken open by a falling star.

**Raised:** c. 1,000 years before year zero (First Age), by [[Atlas]]. **Struck:** year zero (Second Age), by [[Svarog]] — the [[Second Meteor]].

## Story
[[Atlas]] raised walls of almost perfectly flat stone from the ground, as if someone had built a natural fortress the size of a country, with natural gates and arches.

The Second Age began with catastrophe. [[Svarog]] dropped a smaller meteor (some two megatons) on the First Fortress at the very moment the [[Dwarves]] were migrating into it, and blew it open with terrible loss; an entire dwarven order that had split off to go ahead was wiped out. The dwarves remember it as their great wound, the one that only hardened them. The [[Time Keepers]] date all time from it: **year zero is the falling star that landed far in the west with a great roar.**

## Now
The second meteor's site has never been investigated.
