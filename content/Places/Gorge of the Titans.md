> Not a river canyon but an open wound in the earth, reaching the sea.

**Made:** c. 4,000 years before year zero (First Age), by [[Sylvanus]] (also called the Gorge of Doom).

## Story
[[Sylvanus]] cracked the earth where the sky-stone had struck — at [[World's Breach]] — into the Gorge of the Titans. It is an open wound that runs to the sea, so that the sea flows into its lower end and mingles at its foot.

## Connections
- [[World's Breach]] — the impact it was cracked from
- [[First Meteor]] — the sky-stone
- [[Sylvanus]] — its maker
