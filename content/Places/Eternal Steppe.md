> A vast steppe from the interior to the sea — perfect for riding.

**Made:** c. 4,000 years before year zero (First Age), by [[Atlas]], with its hills.

## Story
[[Atlas]] stretched a vast steppe from the interior of the continent to the sea, and raised hills beside it, reaching toward [[Sky Spear]]. Some thousand years later, c. 3,000 years before year zero, the [[Stone Talus]] stood up between those hills and the mountains.

In the first years after year zero Atlas made the [[Centaurs]] here — neutral nomads, with a small evil order of priests, the [[Purebred]], who hold that the union of beast and man makes them superior to every other race. Around year 100 [[Astralis]] made among them the [[Time Keepers]], who watched the sky and drew the first maps and calendars; around year 200 the centaurs beat the [[Centaur Roads]] across the world. Around year 300 [[Sylvanus]] purified them: the centaurs are now good.

## Now
Home of the centaurs, bound to the humans by the [[Eternal Alliance]].
