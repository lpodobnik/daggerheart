**Raised:** c. year 100 (Second Age), by the [[Stone Talus]] ([[Atlas]])

## Story
Around year 100, in the same years the [[Dwarves|dwarves]] left the Bronze Age — learning to meld magic from the [[Great Crystals|crystals]] with [[Star Metal|star metal]] and becoming smiths whose creations blend the two — [[Atlas]]'s [[Stone Talus]] raised a wonder in [[Kharag Duun]]: the Magical Forge, fed with magma from the depths of [[Sky Spear]].
