> A near-perfect ring of mountain holding a deep lake at enormous height — until a god struck it.

**Raised:** c. 1,000 years before year zero (First Age), by [[Atlas]]. **Collapsed:** c. year 640 (Third Age), by [[Astralis]].

## Story
[[Atlas]] raised a near-perfect ring of mountain (he drew the circle drunk) with, instead of a summit, a rift holding a deep lake at enormous height. With the Perpetual Tempest — the [[Right Eye of Atlas]] — it makes the pair.

Around year 640, late in Age III, [[Astralis]], angry that Atlas would suppress magic after the [[Shattering]], struck him in the eye: the Left Eye collapsed on one side and its waters flooded the [[War Zone]] into swamp. The armies stopped knowing friend from foe, and men who had been killing each other hauled each other out of the quicksand.

## Now
Broken on one side; its waters made the War Zone a swamp, where humans and centaurs fight water elves forever.
