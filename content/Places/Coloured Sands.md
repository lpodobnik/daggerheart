# The Coloured Sands

> A small peninsula whose shores are coloured sand — "and there is a reason for it."

**Made:** c. 3,000 years before year zero (First Age), by [[Astralis]].

## Story
[[Astralis]] covered the shores of a small peninsula in coloured sands, "and there is a reason for it."

Around 1,000 years before year zero, as Astralis walked his coloured shore, strange beings came out of the sea: tentacles where the lower half of a skull should be, skin iridescent like the inside of a shell; few, and reproducing by rituals not even they fully understand. Other races call them the [[Trenchborn]]. Their order, the [[Echoes of the Void]], senses that sound, dance and colour together can reach their ancestors, but does not yet know how.

## Now
The Trenchborn's home, left a mystery.
