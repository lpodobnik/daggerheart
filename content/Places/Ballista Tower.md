> A siege engine too big to aim, with a range of half the continent.

**Raised:** c. year 620 (Third Age), by [[Astralis]], at [[Heart of the Plains]]

## Story
Around year 620, in the Age of War, [[Heart of the Plains]] raised the Ballista Tower — too big to aim, with a range of half the continent. [[Atlas]]'s [[Harpies|harpies]] founded [[Divine Strike]] to spot for it from the air.

At the end of the Age, the [[Third Meteor|third meteor]] struck the mountain slopes beside the Ballista's city; the earthquake and mudslides broke the city and cracked the Ballista.

## Now
Cracked and out of action.
