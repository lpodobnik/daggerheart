> An elven city on the coast between the jungle's edge and the mountains, and home port of the marines.

**Founded:** c. year 500 (Second Age), by [[Sylvariel]] ([[Sylvanus]])

## Story
Around year 500, late in the Second Age, [[Sylvariel]] the Dragon founded the Elf Outpost on the coast between the jungle's edge and the mountains. Around year 620, [[Astralis]] founded an order there for war at sea — the [[Elf Outpost Marines]], fighters for water combat.

## Now
The Water Elves' marines are based here.
