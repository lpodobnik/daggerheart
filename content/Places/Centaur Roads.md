> Highways beaten across the world by centaur hooves, built to outlast a thousand years — and in places they simply vanish.

**Made:** c. year 200 (Second Age), by [[Astralis]], through the [[Centaurs]]

## Story
[[Astralis]] had the [[Centaurs|centaurs]] beat roads across the world, marking their progress with **waystations** — highways that will outlast a thousand years, with bridges where rivers were hard to cross. The roads pull them toward particular places: one called *Starlight* [?], one the swamp country where they found an ink for longer-lasting maps, one far away from which no one has yet returned. In places the road simply stops and vanishes.

In the same years, the [[Skybreak]] closed the sky. By around year 500, late in the Second Age, [[Atlas]] saw the roads crowded and filled the gap with the [[Halflings]], small nomad traders of the roads. In those same years the [[Stone Talus]] founded the [[Bazaar]] where the roads cross the river.

## Now
Wonders of the world, with waystations and bridges. The halflings trade along them, and caravans leave the Bazaar in every direction.
