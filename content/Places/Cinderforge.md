> The Fire Genasi city on the northern volcano island — steam, airships, and a cracking crystal caged in coils.

**Founded:** c. year 100 (Second Age), by [[Svarog]]'s avatar ([[Ardora]]) · **Pillars of Magic Harnessing:** c. year 640 (Third Age)

## Story
On the northern island stands [[Ardor]], the volcano whose eruption made the [[Fire Genasi]]. Around year 100, [[Svarog]]'s avatar founded Cinderforge there. Living among geysers and steam vents, the Genasi discovered **engineering** and invented **steam power**, and Svarog founded in Cinderforge the [[Cinderwing Cartel]] — Genasi who build steam-driven airships and set out to explore the world, then built a vast trade network across the continent.

The [[Skybreak]] grounded the Cartel. Around year 500, late in the Age, Svarog sent it a prophecy that one of the two lost crystals governs [[Portals|portals]], and made it their master: [[Portals|portal]] magic grows far stronger near shards of the [[First Meteor|first meteor]].

Around year 600, the Genasi raised an army on their island as the elven warships of the [[Last Tide]] sailed past. After the [[Shattering]] cracked every great crystal, [[Ardora]] had Cinderforge's scholars ring their cracking crystal with the **Pillars of Magic Harnessing** — tall coils that catch the leaking magic and feed it to the city (c. year 640, [[Svarog]]).

## Now
Harnessing its cracked crystal with the Pillars; the Cartel grounded, chasing portals and meteor shards.
