# Glaive Port

> A walled human port on the eastern lake that was conquered, starved, turned to cannibalism, and is now ruled by the masters of the fungal dead.

**Founded:** c. year 400 (Second Age), by [[Humans|humans]] at [[Svarog]]'s command · **Now:** besieged, endlessly

## Story
**Founding.** Around year 400, [[Svarog]] commanded the humans to found Glaive Port on the eastern lake, and in the same years advanced it into a **defensive fortress**. Around year 500 it raised an army — the nearest walled city to the new [[Desert Crystals|desert-crystal]] mines and to the [[Orcs|orcs]].

**The Branded.** In a time of fear, the ruler gathered pirates, prisoners and the worst scum of the docks into the [[Branded]] — necks burned black with hot tar — who defend the city's river and sea by the dirtiest means there are: fire-ships full of people rammed into the enemy, plague corpses over the walls ([[Astralis]], c. year 500).

**The fall (c. year 600).** The [[Hollow Knight]] (then the Doombringer) led two orc armies and their war beasts against Glaive Port and took it — the [[Fall of Glaive Port]], first great battle of the Age. No massacre, no pillage: curfew, martial law, a trading city still trading, its defenders gone partisan. It became the heart of a [[Great Orkish Empire]] in the making ([[Atlas]]). [[Elyndra]] answered by founding the [[Purifiers of Evil]] for a counter-offensive.

**Corruption.** The occupiers found city life better than the desert, and their ruling caste rotted into brutes ([[Astralis]] corrupted the city). [[Sylvanus]] then bound corrupted Glaive Port to the [[Last Tide]] in the [[Evil Alliance]] — the city, not the orcish race.

**Elyndra's defeat and cannibalism (c. year 610).** Among the city's refugees elsewhere rose the fanatical [[Vengeful Paladins]] — *the only good orc is a dead orc* ([[Svarog]]). The [[Hollow Knight]], disgusted that his war had decayed into corruption, abandoned the orcs. [[Elyndra]] led the [[Hands of Elyndra|Healers]] and the Purifiers to liberate the city and was decisively defeated — [[Elyndra's Defeat]]; the pursuers burned part of the [[Fungorn|mushroom forest]], the battlefield became the [[Corpse Field]], the river was rerouted and the lake began to dry. The siege had lasted six or seven years; the giants had frozen the lake, fishing and trade died, and Glaive Port fell to **cannibalism** — [[Svarog]]'s avatar corrupted it again.

**Plague, famine, and the four armies (c. year 620).** The [[Fungal Plague]] was worst here. Glaive Port raised a third army; famine came as the fungus took livestock, fish and crops, and a fourth army rose. All four marched south for bread against the [[Grove]] — the [[Great Battle of the Third Age]] — and were lost: fewer than half died, the rest scattered as bandits across the [[Wasteland]].

**Melakai and the Mycomancers (c. year 630).** A leper healed himself and began to heal others — [[Melakai]] the Messenger, [[Svarog]]'s avatar. He founded the [[Mycomancers]] — humans, orcs, anyone — who command the [[Fungal Zombies|fungal dead]]. They became the ruling class, and the dead now patrol the streets without harming anyone.

**The siege (c. year 640).** [[Melakai]] raised a defensive army. The [[Deepkin]] — for whom raising corpses with fungus is a travesty, and whose forest had burned — laid siege, and the city held: the [[Siege of Glaive Port]], a siege without end ([[Astralis]]). Mycomancer agents slipped out disguised as fungus to seed hidden cells in other cities.

## Now
Mostly human; the orcs are the nominal rulers, the Mycomancers the real ones, through religion. The fungal dead patrol the streets. Besieged without end by the Deepkin. The Branded still hold its waters. Melakai lives here, old and decrepit but alive. Its four armies went out and died. The city may be renamed.
