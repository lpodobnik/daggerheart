# Coldspire

> A city of stone and ice on a raised hill in the cold north — the last refuge of the Ice Giants.

**Founded:** c. year 300 (Second Age), by [[Atlas]] · **Where:** the cold north, near the [[Frozen North]]'s crystal

## Story
Around year 300, [[Atlas]] founded Coldspire in the cold north: stone and ice on a raised hill. Whose city it was at first is not known.

After [[Elyndra's Defeat]] at [[Glaive Port]], what remained of the [[Ice Giants]] withdrew to Coldspire (c. year 620) — now rare, with few healers left of the [[Hands of Elyndra]].

Near Coldspire lies the Frozen North's [[Great Crystals|great crystal]], through which the Ice Giants learned to channel healing magic; since the [[Shattering]] it is cracked.

## Now
Refuge of the last giants, beside a cracked crystal.

## Connections
- [[Ice Giants]] — the remnant who live here
- [[Hands of Elyndra]] — the giants' dwindling healers
- [[Frozen North]] — its land and crystal
