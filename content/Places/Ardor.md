# Ardor

> The volcano on the northern island, where a woman of flame bathes and the earth plays its organ.

**Raised:** c. 4,000 years before year zero (First Age), by [[Svarog]].

## Story
When [[Astralis]] said there wasn't enough fire, [[Svarog]] raised a volcano on the northern island and named it Ardor. Around 3,000 years before year zero his avatar took up residence in it: [[Ardora]], a woman made of flame.

In the same years Ardor erupted, and the glowing stones that fell were quickened by the chaos-energy of the cosmos into the [[Fire Genasi]]. Astralis gave them a gift: where the volcano punctures the earth, vents roar like organ-pipes — "the first sound your race ever heard was the earth playing its organ." He also seeded [[Star Metal]] near the volcano.

Around year 100 Ardora founded [[Cinderforge]] on the northern island. One of the four known [[Great Crystals]] stands there.

## Now
Ardora, "the First Born of the Fire," bathes in her volcano, inaccessible.

## Connections
- [[Ardora]] — lives in it
- [[Fire Genasi]] — born of its eruption
