# The Orc War Camps

> The orcs' camps in the Obsidian Desert — the ones who stayed behind when the armies went to Glaive Port.

**Founded:** c. year 400 (Second Age), by [[Atlas]] · **Where:** the [[Obsidian Desert]]

## Story
[[Atlas]] raised the [[Orcs]] in the desert around year 200 — living for glory and war, with their warrior order the [[Sandborn]] — and around year 300 made them masters of war beasts and raised an army in the south-eastern desert. Around year 400 he founded their War Camps in the [[Obsidian Desert]]. Around year 500 the Doombringer ([[Hollow Knight]]) raised the orcs a second army. Nearby, [[Sylvanus]] made the south-eastern sands grow [[Desert Crystals|crystals]], and the first mines opened.

Around year 600, the Hollow Knight led both armies and their war beasts against [[Glaive Port]] and took it. But the orcs who had stayed in the desert said *that is not the way*: they gathered the magic crystals of the sands and fused them into a great obelisk in their central war camp — the [[Final End]] ([[Atlas]]).

## Now
No armies left. The desert orcs, good but fanatical, keep the Final End.
