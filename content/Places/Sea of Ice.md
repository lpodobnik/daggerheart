# The Sea of Ice

> Far waters frozen thick enough to walk on — and, strangely, a warm sea locked inside them.

**Made:** c. 5,000 years before year zero (First Age), by [[Svarog]].

## Story
[[Svarog]] froze the far waters into a Sea of Ice: sheets thick enough to walk across in the cold months, with a glacier. And, strangely, he left a small **warm sea** enclosed within it.

Some thousand years later, c. 4,000 years before year zero, Svarog set a small island, pleasant and green, in the middle of the warm sea: [[Long Harbour]].

## Connections
- [[Long Harbour]] — the green island in the warm sea
- [[Svarog]] — its maker

Open: [?] nothing is recorded of it after Age I. Whether the ice floes bridging [[Jungle Island]] and the mainland — where the [[Frost Genasi]] arose — belong to this sea is unknown.
