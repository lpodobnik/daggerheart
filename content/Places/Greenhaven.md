> The central city on the River of Life, home of history, writing and the Library — and now held by dwarven wardens.

**Founded:** c. year 300 (Second Age), by [[Sylvanus]] · **The Library:** c. year 400 (Second Age), by [[Atlas]]

## Story
[[Sylvanus]] founded Greenhaven on the [[River of Life]], which flows through the city, mostly [[Humans|human]]. Around year 400, [[Astralis]] advanced it — the central city — with **history and writing**, and [[Atlas]] built a **Library** there. Around year 500, the [[Eternal Alliance]] between humans and centaurs was signed in the central city.

Around year 600, the [[Order of the Eternal Peace]], through the [[Stone Talus]], called humans and [[Centaurs|centaurs]] to arms in Greenhaven. Around year 630 [[Karros and Kassander]] led the armies of [[Heart of the Plains]] and Greenhaven (the "Liberatorians") against the [[Water Elves|water elves]] on their land; it ended even and became the [[War Zone]].

Around year 640 the [[Dwarves|dwarves]], seeing humans become the dominant race, marched with the Stone Talus and took Greenhaven as its wardens — no massacre, nothing else changed ([[Atlas]]). [[Karros and Kassander]] came home from the front, found the dwarves ruling, and turned them to the honourable way ([[Astralis]]). But Atlas raised among those same dwarves a ruling caste, the [[Magisters]], who hold that dwarves are not only the first race but the best, and will do anything to stay in power.

## Now
Dwarf-ruled; purified to the honourable way by Karros and Kassander, yet governed by the Magisters. Which wins remains to be seen.
