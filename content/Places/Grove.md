# The Grove

> A hidden elven city around the Tree of Life, known to no race, where the forest shifts and scouts walk in circles.

**Founded:** c. year 610 (Third Age), by [[Sylvanus]]

## Story
[[Sylvanus]]'s [[Water Elves|elves]] found the [[Tree of Life]] and hid a city around it in the forest of peace, known to no race: the Grove. Around year 620, it turned to magical defence — no walls, but a forest that shifts and scouts who walk in circles; it spreads as small strange places rather than growing. It raised an army of [[Water Druids|water druids]].

Then [[Glaive Port]]'s four starving armies marched south for bread against it — the [[Great Battle of the Third Age]]. The druids confused them, their generals vanished — eaten by fungus or by trees — and with the [[Spider People]] they drove them out; the forest swallowed them. The Grove survived, wounded. East of it, along the road into the desert, lies the [[Wasteland]].

Around year 630 [[Sylvanus]] sent the [[Lizardmen|lizardmen]] through the [[Underdark]] against the Tree; the Grove's misdirection held, its assassins killed their chiefs, and the lizardmen turned on each other.

## Now
Hidden; magical misdirection; water druids. That it exists at all is a secret.
