> A maze of flat-topped mountains above the desert, forever dusted black by the wind.

**Raised:** c. 2,000 years before year zero (First Age), by [[Svarog]].

## Story
[[Svarog]] raised the Black Mesa above the [[Obsidian Desert]]: a maze of flat-topped mountains and a canyon, one bank of the river far above the other, the wind from above forever dusting them black.

## Connections
- [[Obsidian Desert]] — the desert below it
- [[Svarog]] — its maker
