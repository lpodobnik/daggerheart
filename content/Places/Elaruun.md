# Elaruun

> The dragon's island, behind the winds — where a great crystal hangs in the air.

## Story
Elaruun is one of the islands of [[Sylvanus]]'s [[Sylmara Archipelago]]. Around 2,000 years before year zero Sylvanus's avatar appeared here — [[Sylvariel]] the Dragon — in the same years that the [[Water Elves]] were made.

At the [[Coming of Magic]] (year zero) one of the four known [[Great Crystals]] appeared on Elaruun, behind the winds. Around year 400 Sylvanus founded [[Selvaruun]], the City of Scholars, on the dragon's western island. At the [[Shattering]] (c. year 630) one crystal broke and the rest — this one among them — cracked.

## Now
[[Sylvariel]] is on Elaruun. [[Selvaruun]] stands; the crystal is cracked, and the cracks are spreading.
