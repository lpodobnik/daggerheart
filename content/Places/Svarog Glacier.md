# The Svarog Glacier

> Mountains and a glacier above a great north-western bay, melting for years now.

**Made:** c. 1,000 years before year zero (First Age), by [[Svarog]].

*Name:* the table's "Yokul Sleta" was a mangled transcription; Luka renamed it the Svarog Glacier (2026-09-24).

## Story
[[Svarog]] set mountains and a glacier above a great north-western bay. It has been melting for years now from a warmth out of the north, pouring hundreds of streams into rivers and tarns and calving bergs into the sea. It may be what carved the bay.
