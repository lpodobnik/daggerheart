# The Slithering River

> Svarog's river, loosed from a spring inside Atlas's mountain.

**Made:** c. 5,000 years before year zero (First Age), by [[Svarog]].

## Story
[[Svarog]] tapped a spring inside [[Atlas]]'s mountain, [[Sky Spear]], and loosed the Slithering River down to a great delta on the coast. Atlas answered by opening the other side of the mountain: a sister river ran the opposite way, into Svarog's arid land, and over the rim of [[World's Breach]] as the Breach Falls.

## Connections
- [[Sky Spear]] — its source
- [[World's Breach]] — its sister river's falls
