> The world beneath the world, opened in the south.

**Opened:** c. year 200 (Second Age), by [[Sylvanus]], in the south.

## Story
Already in Age I the Breach Falls sank through the fissured floor of [[World's Breach]] into the Underdark. Around year 200 [[Sylvanus]] opened it.

Around year 630 Sylvanus sent the [[Lizardmen]] through the Underdark against the [[Tree of Life]]. The [[Grove]]'s misdirection held, its assassins killed their chiefs, and the lizardmen turned on each other; the [[Hollow Knight]] built them a second war camp, and they split into two warring factions around the Underdark mouth on [[Jungle Island]].

Around year 640 [[Elyndra]] gathered an army to capture it, and with the [[Water Elves]] took Jungle Island's Underdark mouth. The lizardmen were broken but not destroyed.

## On the map
Two mouths are marked: on the mainland at the [[Gorge of the Titans]]' sea mouth, and on [[Jungle Island]]'s north-east shore. Underground passages run between them beneath the [[Sea of Ice]]'s floes — the lizardmen's road to the [[Tree of Life]].

## Now
The elves hold Jungle Island's mouth. At the mainland mouth camp **L1** and **L2** — the remnants of the two lizardman armies that came through, turned on each other — beside the [[Tree of Life]] they failed to take. The war camps on [[Jungle Island]] still stand.
