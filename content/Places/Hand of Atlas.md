> Five volcanic fingers thrust up out of the sea — four fingers and a thumb, the palm beneath the waves.

**Raised:** c. 6,000 years before year zero (First Age), by [[Atlas]] (also called the Land of Hills).

## Story
[[Atlas]]'s first work: five volcanic fingers rising out of the sea, the palm hidden below. Around 4,000 years before year zero he set the Perpetual Tempest in its bay — hurricane winds, wild storm, forever — the [[Right Eye of Atlas]].

## Now
Nothing recorded since Age I; the tempest still blows in the bay.

## Connections
- [[Atlas]] — its maker
- [[Right Eye of Atlas]] — the storm in its bay
