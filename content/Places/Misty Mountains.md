# The Misty Mountains

> Mountains that fall sheer into a fog-bound sea, like Velebit. No great city will ever stand there.

**Raised:** c. 1,000 years before year zero (First Age), by [[Svarog]].

## Story
Along the coast of the [[Eternal Fog]], [[Svarog]] raised the Misty Mountains, falling sheer into the sea.

Around year 500, late in Age II, [[Astralis]] raised the [[Spider People]] in the southern mountains — some centaur-like on a spider's body, some humanoid with extra eyes, ranked by how truly spider they are. Their order, the [[Threadspinners]], plays silk strings to speak and pass on knowledge.

Around year 610 Astralis allied the [[Water Elves]] and the Spider People — both neutral, a Switzerland — trading sea-lore for the paths through the Misty Mountains. Some ten years later, around year 620, the Spider People helped the [[Grove]] drive out [[Glaive Port]]'s armies in the [[Great Battle of the Third Age]].

## Now
The Spider People keep their webs and paths here, allied with the water elves.
