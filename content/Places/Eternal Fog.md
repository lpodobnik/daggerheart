> A whole coast wrapped in fog — you cannot see the hand in front of your face.

**Made:** c. 2,000 years before year zero (First Age), by [[Astralis]], in the south.

## Story
[[Astralis]] wrapped a whole coast in eternal fog. Some thousand years later, c. 1,000 years before year zero, [[Svarog]] raised the [[Misty Mountains]] along the fog-bound coast, falling sheer into the sea like Velebit; no great city will ever stand there.

## Connections
- [[Misty Mountains]] — rise along its coast
- [[Astralis]] — its maker
