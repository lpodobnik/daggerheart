> A great obelisk of fused desert crystals in the orcs' central war camp.

**Raised:** c. year 600 (Third Age), by [[Atlas]] · **Where:** the central camp of the [[Orc War Camps]]

## Story
When the Hollow Knight's orcs took [[Glaive Port]] and began to rot there, the [[Orcs|orcs]] who had stayed in the desert said *that is not the way*. They set out to study the magic crystals of the sands — the [[Desert Crystals|desert crystals]], small, obsidian-like, that amplify magic — gathered them, and fused them into a great obelisk in their central war camp: the Final End.

## Now
The Final End amplifies magic, and no one understands it.
