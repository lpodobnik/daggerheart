# Jungle Island

> The great southern island, all of it jungle — and now a war with three sides.

**Made:** c. 4,000 years before year zero (jungle), finished c. 2,000 years before year zero (First Age), by [[Sylvanus]].

## Story
[[Sylvanus]] grew jungle on the great southern island c. 4,000 years before year zero, and some thousand years later, c. 2,000 years before year zero, finished it: all of it jungle.

**The lizardmen.** Around year 100 [[Atlas]] made the [[Lizardmen]] here in Sylvanus's jungles — evil, wholly tribal, ruled by [[Atlas's First]], nobles who each killed every sibling from their own clutch of eggs. [[Sylvariel]] slipped among them and founded the [[Arcane Fang]], usurpers of magic who want every crystal for their sons. Around year 200 Sylvanus gave them a "city" — not a city but a **chain of jungle villages**, a civilisation of the Amazon. In the same years the [[Frost Genasi]] arose on the island amid the ice floes that bridge Jungle Island and the mainland, and Sylvanus opened the [[Underdark]] in the south.

**The war camps.** Around year 630 the lizardmen took up arms (Sylvanus) and the [[Hollow Knight]] raised them a second army (Atlas). Sylvanus sent them through the Underdark against the [[Tree of Life]]; the [[Grove]]'s misdirection held, its assassins killed their chiefs, and the lizardmen turned on each other. The Hollow Knight built them a second **war camp** (the camps are L1 and L2 on the map), and they split into two warring factions around the **Underdark mouth**. [[Sylvariel]] raised a [[Water Elves|water elf]] army against them.

**The mouth taken.** Around year 640 [[Elyndra]] gathered an army to open a front against the lizardmen; she and the water elves together took Jungle Island's Underdark mouth. The lizardmen were broken but not destroyed.

## Now
The island's war camps still stand, and the lizardmen are split in two and at war with each other; the elves hold the island's Underdark mouth. Across the water, the remnants of the two armies that went through the Underdark camp at the mainland mouth, fighting each other.
