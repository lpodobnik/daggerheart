> A small island of beautiful forest far in the north-west — the Last Tide's home, where the great crystal shattered.

**Raised:** c. 1,000 years before year zero (First Age), by [[Sylvanus]]. — the small forested island with the scribbled-out crystal and the red X.

## Story
Around 1,000 years before year zero, late in the First Age, [[Sylvanus]] set a small island of beautiful forest far in the north-west: Nyravel (the log: *otok daleko na SZ sa šumom*). It is one of the islands of the [[Sylmara Archipelago]], with [[Elaruun]].

A party of [[Explorers]] who reached it turned inward and became the [[Last Tide]]. In the first years after year zero Sylvanus founded their city on the island: [[Nerathis]]. With the [[Coming of Magic]] one of the four known [[Great Crystals]] appeared here; at the [[Shattering]] (c. year 630) it was the one that broke.

## Now
Wild magic and [[Monstrosities|monstrosities]], densest here; the corrupted, decadent Last Tide and their navy in [[Nerathis]].
