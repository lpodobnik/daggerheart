# Nerathis

> The secluded elves' city and shipyard of the warships of doom — on the island where the great crystal shattered.

**Founded:** c. year zero (Second Age), by [[Sylvanus]] · **Where:** [[Nyravel]], the forested island far in the north-west, in the [[Sylmara Archipelago]]

## Story
A party of [[Explorers]] who reached a far place turned inward and became the [[Last Tide]] — secluded, xenophobic, evil. In the first years after year zero, [[Sylvanus]] founded a first city for them: Nerathis. Around year 300 [[Svarog]] corrupted it, and Sylvanus advanced it into a shipyard — **warships of doom**, for the secluded elves mean to rule the seas.

Around year 600, the Last Tide raided every settlement along the rich northern shores and took a foothold, and Sylvanus bound them to [[Glaive Port]] in the [[Evil Alliance]]. Around year 610 generations of inbreeding cost the elves a whole generation's shipbuilding ([[Astralis]]): their raiders are small fast boats of twenty, and for a while there will be no new ones.

Around year 630 [[Atlas]]'s [[Shattering]] broke the great crystal on [[Nyravel]], the Last Tide's island — chosen, it is said, by chance. Around year 640 the elves, grown arrogant and decadent, were corrupted wholesale ([[Svarog]]).

## Now
Wild magic and [[Monstrosities|monstrosities]], densest here; a decadent, corrupted elf navy that can't build new ships for a generation.
