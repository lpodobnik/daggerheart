> A forest that is not a forest: towering giant mushrooms, where strange things happen.

**Made:** c. 2,000 years before year zero (First Age), by [[Svarog]].

## Story
Where dry northern air meets southern damp, cut by a mountain, [[Svarog]] grew a wood of towering giant mushrooms of enormous diversity — Fungorn — where strange things happen.

Around year 100 [[Astralis]] raised a new people here from the mixing of [[Dwarves|dwarf]] explorers and [[Trenchborn]] explorers: short and stout, with bearded tentacles — the [[Deepkin]]. Their shamans, the [[Bent Eye]], eat the mushrooms and build non-Euclidean structures no one has yet seen; whoever sees one, the story goes, goes absolutely insane.

Around year 610, after [[Elyndra's Defeat]] at [[Glaive Port]], her pursuers burned part of the mushroom forest. Around year 620 a [[Fungal Plague]] spread out of the burned forest (Svarog) — scholars think it the fungi's own defence; the mushroom-folk, who breed by spores, are immune. Around year 630 the Deepkin raised an army (Astralis), since raising corpses with fungus is a travesty and it was their forest that burned; around year 640 they laid siege to Glaive Port.

## Now
Partly burned. Its Deepkin besiege Glaive Port without end.
