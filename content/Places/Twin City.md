# The Twin City

> A fortress that exists only to make soldiers: everyone in the region serves a year on the wall.

**Built:** c. year 600 (Third Age), by [[Sylvariel]] ([[Sylvanus]])

## Story
At the opening of the Age of War, [[Sylvariel]] the Dragon built the Twin City to control the pass between two mountains — a city that exists purely to hold that pass, and to make soldiers. Everyone in the region serves a year on the wall. Its people are mostly [[Water Elves|elves]], with some [[Humans|humans]].

The [[Goblins]]' [[Marrow Munchers]] riddled both mountains with holes big enough for small creatures to get through — raiders pass, armies don't.

## Now
Still a fortress that exists to make soldiers.
