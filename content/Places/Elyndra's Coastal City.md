> The city a wounded angel built on the coast after her army died at Glaive Port.

**Built:** c. year 620 (Third Age), by [[Elyndra]] ([[Sylvanus]])

## Story
After [[Elyndra's Defeat|her defeat]] at [[Glaive Port]], [[Elyndra]], wounded, built a city on the coast below the forest of peace. In the same years, what remained of the [[Ice Giants]] withdrew to [[Coldspire]]. Elyndra fought on: around year 640 she gathered an army against the [[Lizardmen|lizardmen]] and, with the [[Water Elves|water elves]], took [[Jungle Island]]'s [[Underdark]] mouth.
