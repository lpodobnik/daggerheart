## 2 · The Story So Far

### The First Age — The Shaping

**The world was born in water and violence.** [[Sylvanus]] raised an archipelago in the western sea — **[[Sylmara Archipelago|Sylmara]]** — shaped it, and set permanent winds about it that drive any sailor away, so that the islands are almost impossible to reach. [[Svarog]], being chaos, let chaos choose his place and struck the near-centre of the continent with something from the sky: a crater as deep as a canyon and perfectly round, riddled with tunnels and cavities and perhaps once far deeper than anyone now knows, which the races would one day call **[[World's Breach]]**; around it he baked the land into a desert where no moisture survives. [[Astralis]] flooded a great region with lakes and at their heart set one shaped like a five-pointed star — **[[Star Lake]]** — and, on Sylmara, grew a bamboo like no other: waterproof yet hard and supple, with as yet no one to use it. And [[Atlas]] thrust five volcanic fingers up out of the sea — four fingers and a thumb, the palm beneath the waves — **The [[Hand of Atlas]]**.

**Then the mountains rose.** [[Atlas]] raised **[[Sky Spear|Sky Spear Mountain]]** — a massif that climbs far higher than it spreads, terribly steep, its summit a stone spear piercing the clouds. [[Sylvanus]] raised a range of splitting mountains that isolate a region, with a single good pass through them. [[Svarog]] tapped a spring inside Atlas's mountain and loosed the **[[Slithering River]]** down to a great delta on the coast. [[Astralis]] planted a coast that "looks unnatural": a forest of obsidian spires some fifteen metres tall that makes the shore unapproachable and reflects light, day and night, casting an uncanny glow. Atlas answered Svarog by opening the other side of his mountain, so that a sister river burst forth and ran the other way into Svarog's arid land — narrowing from a torrent to a wide stream and pouring over the rim of [[World's Breach]] in one of the most spectacular waterfalls any race will ever see; that side of the crater is forever wrapped in mist, and the water never forms a lake but sinks through the fissured floor into the [[Underdark]]. Sylvanus added a huge lake and a river to a second delta near the unnatural coast. And Svarog froze the far waters into a **[[Sea of Ice]]** — sheets thick enough to walk across in the cold months, with a glacier — and, strangely, left a small **warm sea** enclosed within it.

**Around 4,000 years before year zero, the land filled.** [[Atlas]] set a **[[Right Eye of Atlas|Perpetual Tempest]]** just south of the [[Hand of Atlas]], rising out of the sea — hurricane winds, wild storm, forever. It is the **Right Eye of Atlas**, and with the ring-mountain lake it makes the pair. [[Sylvanus]] cracked the earth where the sky-stone had struck into the **[[Gorge of the Titans]]** — not a river canyon but an open wound reaching the sea, so that the sea flows into its lower end and mingles at its foot. [[Svarog]] set a small island, pleasant and green, in the middle of his warm sea: **[[Long Harbour]]**. Astralis laid a swamp that thickens into true forest, hot-and-wet turning merely wet, with tar pits in the marsh. Atlas stretched a vast steppe from the interior to the sea — the **[[Eternal Steppe]]** — and raised hills beside it, reaching toward [[Sky Spear]]. Sylvanus grew jungle on the great southern island. And when [[Astralis]] said there wasn't enough fire, Svarog raised a volcano on the northern island and named it **[[Ardor]]**.

**Around 3,000 years before year zero came the first living things.** [[Atlas]] made an avatar: between the hills and the mountains stood **The [[Stone Talus]]**. [[Sylvanus]] set two mountain lakes whose rivers merge and run down to a great delta, with jungle about its lower reaches. [[Svarog]] answered Atlas with an avatar of his own: in the volcano [[Ardor]] lives **[[Ardora]]**, a woman made of flame. [[Astralis]] covered the shores of a small peninsula in [[Coloured Sands|coloured sands]], "and there is a reason for it." Then Atlas commanded his Talus: lightning gathered at the spear-tip of [[Sky Spear]] and struck the Talus, and the pieces that fell became proto-[[Dwarves|dwarves]]. So the **Dwarves** were made, who named themselves **The Children of Stone** and went to live where dwarves live — inside Sky Spear Mountain — and among them, because Atlas is a god of war, arose a sect of warriors with crude stone weapons: **The [[Stone Defenders]]**. Svarog would not be outdone: Ardor erupted, and the glowing stones that fell were quickened by the chaos-energy of the cosmos into the **[[Fire Genasi]]** — burning humanoids, evil, who also claim the name Children of Stone (who came first is a matter of dispute). Obsessed with bloodline, they keep a small order of those descended from the very first: **[[Ardora's First]]**, their nobility. Astralis gave the Fire Genasi a gift: where the volcano punctures the earth, vents roar like organ-pipes — "the first sound your race ever heard was the earth playing its organ." And Astralis seeded **[[Star Metal]]** into the rock of Sky Spear (and near the volcano) — the dwarves cannot see it, but they *feel* it glinting in the fissures, and it is one reason they never left the place they were born.

**c. 2,000 years before year zero.** [[Atlas]] spread a great desert across the south-east; with [[Svarog]]'s arid crater-land it became the **[[Obsidian Desert]]** — not merely black sand, but sand of ground obsidian, volcanic glass. [[Sylvanus]]'s avatar appeared on [[Elaruun]] — **[[Sylvariel]] the Dragon** — and Sylvanus made the **[[Water Elves]]**: skilled, neutral, spreading through the islands along the cold currents, the bamboo letting them build rafts; their first order are **The [[Explorers]]**, who mean to find what lies on far unknown shores. Svarog grew a forest that is not a forest: where dry northern air meets southern damp, cut by a mountain, a wood of towering giant mushrooms of enormous diversity — **[[Fungorn]]** — where strange things happen. [[Astralis]] raised the **[[Marble Mountains]]** on a peninsula: regular, almost geometric forms of marble. Atlas commanded the [[Stone Talus|Talus]] again, and above the star-metal deposit the [[Dwarves|dwarves]] built **[[Kharag Duun]]**, "the foundation of the mountain," taking the deposit as a sign that the powers had made a home for them; they worship the Talus and the one who made him. Sylvanus finished the southern island: all of it jungle. Svarog raised the **[[Black Mesa]]** above the desert — a maze of flat-topped mountains and a canyon, one bank of the river far above the other, the wind from above forever dusting them black. Astralis laid the **[[Great Plains]]** — soil so rich that whatever you plant is up the next day, watered from below. Atlas, watching what the Talus had made and what Astralis had made, felt his heart of conflict soften, and drew a river to feed the plains: **The [[River of Life]]**. Svarog made the tip of his peninsula a fiery land of geysers and hot, spitting mud. And Astralis wrapped a whole coast in **[[Eternal Fog|eternal fog]]** — you cannot see the hand in front of your face.

**c. 1,000 years before year zero, and the end of the Age.** One day a shape rose out of [[Star Lake]] — humanoid, though there were no humanoids yet, silver, with no face and no sex: **[[Astralis]]'s avatar**. [[Atlas]] raised two enormous cliffs with a gap between them, and the sea ran in to fill an inland sea between the two peaks — **The [[Twins]]**. [[Sylvanus]] made the far end of the world hilly and bitterly cold — snow, ice, the pole: the **[[Frozen North]]** (the silver avatar wandered there and was snowed on). [[Svarog]] carved the **[[Badlands]]** — razor-sharp limestone karst where ancient rivers washed everything soft away, leaving blades of stone, pits and sinkholes; an avatar can cross it, ordinary folk barely. Astralis fixed the sky above Star Lake: always clear; nothing can cloud it, only wind. Atlas raised a near-perfect ring of mountain (he drew the circle drunk) with, instead of a summit, a rift holding a deep lake at enormous height — the **[[Left Eye of Atlas]]** ([[Right Eye of Atlas|the Right Eye]] is an earlier work of his). Sylvanus set a small island of beautiful forest far in the north-west: **[[Nyravel]]**. Svarog set mountains and a glacier above a great north-western bay — **the [[Svarog Glacier]]**, melting for years now from a warmth out of the north, pouring hundreds of streams into rivers and tarns and calving bergs into the sea; it may be what carved the bay. Then, as Astralis walked his [[Coloured Sands|coloured shore]], strange beings came out of the sea: where the lower half of a skull should be, tentacles; skin iridescent like the inside of a shell; few, and reproducing by rituals not even they fully understand. Other races call them the **[[Trenchborn]]**. They are evil. Their order, **[[Echoes of the Void]]**, senses that sound, dance and colour together can reach their ancestors — whatever that means — but does not yet know how. Atlas raised walls of almost perfectly flat stone from the ground, as if someone had built a natural fortress the size of a country, with natural gates and arches — **The [[First Fortress]]**. [[Sylvariel]] moved among the elves: a party of [[Explorers]] who reached a far place turned inward and became secluded xenophobes — evil — who want only to be left alone: **The [[Last Tide]]**. And along the fog-bound coast Svarog raised the **[[Misty Mountains]]**, which fall sheer into the sea like Velebit; no great city will ever stand there.

So ended the First Age.

### The Second Age — The Coming of the Races

**It began as the First Age did: with a stone from the sky.** But this time it was catastrophe. [[Svarog]] dropped a smaller meteor (some two megatons) on the [[First Fortress]] at the very moment the [[Dwarves|dwarves]] were migrating into it, and blew it open with terrible loss; an entire dwarven order that had split off to go ahead was wiped out. The dwarves remember it as their great wound, the one that only hardened them. The [[Time Keepers]] would later date all time from this: **year zero is the falling star that landed far in the west with a great roar.** Then the gods went looking for worshippers. [[Astralis]] concluded that the only way to multiply the faithful was to do the opposite of everything he'd made so far: the most basic, featureless beings — but *many*, who breed easily and gladly and spread. They named themselves **[[Humans]]**: neutral, born on the [[Great Plains]] along the [[River of Life]], their first order one of pure survival and peace — **The [[Cradle]]** — having met no one yet. [[Atlas]] made the **[[Centaurs]]** on the [[Eternal Steppe]], which is perfect for riding: neutral nomads, but with a small evil order of priests, **The [[Purebred]]**, who hold that the union of beast and man makes them superior to every other race. Then [[Sylvanus]] changed the world: in four places **floating crystals appeared in the air**, and in time the races will learn that these are what fuels magic. **Magic had entered the world.** And Sylvanus founded a first city for his secluded elves: **[[Nerathis]]**.

**The years around year 100 were the busiest yet.** [[Svarog]]'s avatar founded **[[Cinderforge]]**, the [[Fire Genasi]] city on the northern island. [[Astralis]] went among [[Atlas]]'s [[Centaurs|centaurs]] and made **The [[Time Keepers]]**: a women-led order who found that by watching the sky they could align their cycles and rituals — and who, watching the night sky, drew the first maps of the world and made the first **calendars**. The [[Dwarves|dwarves]] left the Bronze Age: they learned to **meld magic from the crystals with [[Star Metal|star metal]]**, becoming smiths whose creations blend the two. Out of the [[Frozen North]] [[Sylvanus]] brought the **[[Ice Giants]]** — good healers — whose order, **The [[Hands of Elyndra]]**, learned through the crystal there to channel healing magic. The Fire Genasi, living among geysers and steam vents, discovered **engineering** and invented **steam power**. In [[Fungorn]] Astralis raised a new people from the mixing of dwarf explorers and [[Trenchborn]] explorers: short and stout, with bearded tentacles — the **[[Deepkin]]** — whose shamans, **The [[Bent Eye]]**, eat the mushrooms and build non-Euclidean structures no one has yet seen; the story goes that whoever sees one goes absolutely insane. Atlas made the **[[Lizardmen]]** in Sylvanus's jungles on the southern island: evil, wholly tribal, ruled by **[[Atlas's First]]** — the nobles, each of whom became so by killing every sibling from his own clutch of eggs. [[Sylvariel]] slipped among the lizardmen and founded a sect of usurpers of magic who want every crystal for their sons: **The [[Arcane Fang]]**. Svarog founded, in Cinderforge, **The [[Cinderwing Cartel]]** — Fire Genasi who build steam-driven airships and set out to explore the world. Astralis founded, among the [[Water Elves|water elves]], **The [[Astral Chord]]**: they discovered that the special bamboo, bent and poured just so, gives tones so beautiful they stun any listener not used to them; the instruments must be made in complete silence, and the notes were learned by watching the stars. Atlas's [[Stone Talus|Talus]] raised a wonder in [[Kharag Duun]]: the **[[Magical Forge]]**, fed with magma from the mountain's depths. And Svarog commanded his Cartel to build a **vast trade network across the continent** — and to see off any competition.

**c. year 200.** On the southern island [[Sylvanus]] gave the [[Lizardmen|lizardfolk]] a "city" — not a city but a chain of jungle villages, a civilisation of the Amazon. The [[Cinderwing Cartel]], trading far and wide, came to the island that sits amid the ice floes bridging [[Jungle Island]] and the mainland, where a splinter arose: after a mutiny on an airship they renounced fire, embraced ice, and lost their cinder to the cold — the **[[Frost Genasi]]**, on bad terms with the Cartel's centre. [[Astralis]] had the [[Centaurs|centaurs]] beat **roads** across the world, marking their progress with **waystations** — highways that will outlast a thousand years, with bridges where rivers were hard to cross; the roads pull them toward particular places (one called *Starlight*, one the swamp country where they found an ink for longer-lasting maps, one far away from which no one has yet returned), and in places the road simply stops and vanishes. [[Atlas]] made a second avatar: out of the sands of the [[Obsidian Desert]] an empty suit of obsidian plate stood up — **The [[Hollow Knight|Doombringer]]** (later called **the Hollow Knight**). Then Sylvanus closed the sky: **The [[Skybreak]]**, magical storms that prevent all flying travel — the knowledge is not lost, but nothing may fly. [[Svarog]] raised the **[[Goblins]]** out of the south-eastern mountains — evil raiders whose priests are **The [[Marrow Munchers]]**. Atlas raised the **[[Orcs]]** in the desert — living for glory and war, with their order of warriors, **The [[Sandborn]]**. And Sylvanus opened the **[[Underdark]]**.

**Around year 300 — the [[Void Glance]].** [[Astralis]] opened a **ley-line portal** — a brief glimpse into the void, felt across the whole world. Its epicentre was near the [[Twins]], and everyone close to it was marked by how they met it. Those within the crater's radius simply ceased, taken into a cosmic horror that left nothing to bury. Those who watched it with their own eyes lived, but carry ever after a sickness the world would name **the Void Glance**: they see everything in broken, multidimensional angles no one else can follow. And those far enough that the event reached them as sound alone — among them a band of [[Humans|humans]] — found patterns in what they had heard and set those patterns down as hymns: dissonant, terrible choral music that stops a listener where they stand. They became **The [[Star Chorus]]**, and their mission is to teach it to anyone who wishes to learn. The [[Goblins|goblins]], whose mountains lie nearby, were simply among those caught — nothing special marks them out. When it was over, the epicentre had been left as a crater in the shape of a **five-pointed star** — not a star itself, but bearing its shape, as [[Star Lake]] does. [[World's Breach]], far to the west, was untouched by it. Elsewhere in those years: [[Atlas]] advanced the [[Orcs|orcs]] into **masters of war beasts**; [[Sylvanus]] **purified the [[Centaurs|centaurs]] — they are now good**; [[Svarog]] made the **[[Skjaldborg]]** in the sea; Atlas **raised an army** in the south-eastern desert; Sylvanus founded **[[Greenhaven]]** on the [[River of Life]], mostly human; Svarog **corrupted** [[Nerathis]]; Atlas founded **[[Coldspire]]** in the cold north, stone and ice on a raised hill, though for which people is not known; and Sylvanus advanced Nerathis into a shipyard — **warships of doom**, for the secluded elves mean to rule the seas.

**c. year 400.** [[Astralis]]'s second avatar appeared in the centre of the continent — **[[Karros and Kassander|Karros]]**, a good [[Dwarves|dwarf]], riding the [[Centaurs|centaur]] **Kassander** — and began to act. [[Atlas]] made the **[[Harpies]]** in the south of [[Sky Spear]] — winged bird-women, good — with their order, the **[[Sisters of Battle]]**. [[Sylvanus]] made a second avatar: **[[Elyndra]] the Ice Angel**, female, good. [[Svarog]] founded a human order, **The [[Flaming Fist]]** — good, and yes, founded by the god of chaos; no one knows why. Astralis founded **[[Heart of the Plains]]**, the human city. Atlas founded the [[Orcs|orcs]]' **War Camps** in the [[Obsidian Desert]]. Sylvanus founded **[[Selvaruun]]**, the City of Scholars, on **[[Elaruun]]**. Svarog commanded the [[Humans|humans]] to found **[[Glaive Port]]** on the eastern lake. Astralis advanced [[Greenhaven]] — the central city — with **history and writing**, and Atlas built a **Library** there. Sylvanus founded a human order of **[[Settlers]]** (good) to find a site for a sister city. And Svarog advanced Glaive Port into a **defensive fortress**.

**c. year 500, the Second Age's last years.** Some hundred years remained before the Age closed. [[Atlas]] saw the [[Centaurs|centaur]] roads crowded and the sky closed, and filled the gap with a people who belong nowhere and everywhere: the **[[Halflings]]**, small nomad traders of the roads, neutral, and among them the **[[Kamatari]]**, an order of moneylenders. [[Sylvanus]] made the south-eastern desert grow **crystals** — small, obsidian-like, born of hot sand and dry air, that amplify magic; the first mines opened and they became very valuable. And the gods admitted that the four [[Great Crystals|great crystals]] are not all of them: **there are six**, and where the other two lie no one knows — not even the gods. [[Glaive Port]], the nearest walled city to the new mines and to the [[Orcs|orcs]], raised an army ([[Svarog]]). [[Astralis]] raised the **[[Spider People]]** in the southern mountains: some centaur-like on a spider's body, some humanoid with extra eyes, ranked by how truly spider they are. They click and build vast silk webs, and no one has yet managed to speak with them; their order, **the [[Threadspinners]]** (*Svirači na nitima*), plays silk strings to speak and to pass on knowledge. The [[Hollow Knight|Doombringer]] raised the orcs a second army. [[Elyndra]] readied the [[Ice Giants]] — a defensive army of ramparts and self-defence. Svarog sent the grounded [[Cinderwing Cartel]] a prophecy: one of the two lost crystals governs **[[Portals|portals]]**, and they were to learn everything of portals, other dimensions and time. Then he made them masters of it: they found that **shards of the [[First Meteor|first meteor]]** — the one that made [[World's Breach]] — make portal magic far stronger nearby, and that far from the shards it is almost impossible. Nobody knows what the meteor was. Astralis bound [[Humans|humans]] and centaurs in the **[[Eternal Alliance]]**, signed in the central city for shared prosperity and defence, and from it came a new, good sub-race: **[[Shapeshifting Centaurs|shapeshifting centaurs]]**, who by ritual and magic can take human form for a while. Atlas founded among them **the [[Order of the Eternal Peace]]**, sworn to keep the alliance from ever breaking, by any means necessary. In Glaive Port, in a time of fear, the ruler gathered pirates, prisoners and the worst scum of the docks into **the [[Branded]]** — necks burned black with hot tar — who defend the city's river and sea by the dirtiest means there are: fire-ships full of people rammed into the enemy, plague corpses over the walls (Astralis). Atlas's [[Stone Talus]] founded a city where the roads cross the river, with no walls at all: **the [[Bazaar]]**, one enormous market where halflings, centaurs, humans, giants and [[Dwarves|dwarves]] trade and caravans leave in every direction. [[Sylvariel]] founded the **[[Elf Outpost]]** on the coast between the jungle's edge and the mountains. And Svarog had the [[Frost Genasi]] give up their igloos and build a real city on their island.

So ended the Second Age.

### The Third Age — The Age of War

*The First Age lasted some six thousand years, the Second some six hundred; the Third some fifty years. It was an age of armies.*

**c. year 600 — the fall of [[Glaive Port]].** The [[Last Tide]], with their warships, raided every settlement along the rich northern shores and took a foothold ([[Sylvanus]]). The Genasi raised an army as the elven ships sailed past their island ([[Svarog]]). [[Karros and Kassander]] raised an army in [[Heart of the Plains]] ([[Astralis]]); the [[Order of the Eternal Peace]], through the [[Stone Talus]], called [[Humans|humans]] and [[Centaurs|centaurs]] to arms in [[Greenhaven]] ([[Atlas]]). [[Sylvariel]] built **the [[Twin City]]** to control the pass between two mountains — a fortress that exists only to make soldiers, where everyone in the region serves a year on the wall; elves mostly, and some humans. The [[Goblins|goblins]], walled out, sent their boldest to dig: **the [[Marrow Munchers]]**, smeared in slime from the fallen star as war paint, turned the mountains to Swiss cheese — raiding parties can pass, armies cannot (Svarog). Then the [[Hollow Knight|Doombringer]], now called **the Hollow Knight**, led two orc armies and their war beasts against Glaive Port, and took it. No massacre, no pillage: curfew, martial law, a trading city still trading, its defenders gone partisan. It became the heart of a **[[Great Orkish Empire]]** in the making (Atlas). [[Elyndra]] answered by founding among the [[Ice Giants]] a good order for a counter-offensive, **the [[Purifiers of Evil]]** (Sylvanus). But the occupiers found city life better than the desert, and their ruling caste rotted into brutes (Astralis corrupted the city). The [[Orcs|orcs]] who had stayed in the desert said *that is not the way*: they gathered the magic crystals of the sands and fused them into a great obelisk in their central war camp, **the [[Final End]]** (Atlas). And Sylvanus bound corrupted Glaive Port to the Last Tide in an **[[Evil Alliance]]** — the city, not the orcish race.

**c. year 610.** Among [[Glaive Port]]'s refugees in other cities rose a fanatical order of [[Vengeful Paladins|vengeful paladins]], a god of suffering at its heart, recruiting among the poor — *the only good orc is a dead orc*. It believes itself the ultimate good and is, in practice, evil ([[Svarog]]). On the [[Last Tide]]'s island, generations of inbreeding cost the elves a whole generation's shipbuilding — their raiders are small fast boats of twenty, and for a while there will be no new ones ([[Astralis]]). [[Sylvanus]] raised **the [[Tree of Life]]** on the plains near [[Heart of the Plains]], below the meteor bay: a vast tree in a Lothlórien forest, the land around it fertile, and the strongest healing magic in the world. The [[Hollow Knight]], disgusted that his war had decayed into corruption, **abandoned the [[Orcs|orcs]]** and went to the [[Harpies|harpies]]; the [[Sisters of Battle]] became holy warriors, sworn to fight injustice everywhere ([[Atlas]]). [[Elyndra]] led the Healers and the Purifiers to liberate Glaive Port and was **decisively defeated**. Her army was destroyed and she escaped; the pursuers burned part of the mushroom forest, the river was rerouted and the lake began to dry, and the battlefield became **the [[Corpse Field]]** of the [[Ice Giants]] (Sylvanus). The siege had lasted six or seven years; the giants had frozen the lake, fishing and trade died, and Glaive Port fell to **cannibalism** — Svarog's avatar corrupted it again. Atlas founded an order of samaritans, of many races and bound to no city, who give up their possessions to help wherever there is ruin: **the [[Vow of Peace]]**. Sylvanus's elves found the Tree and hid a city around it, known to no race: **the [[Grove]]**. Astralis allied the [[Water Elves|water elves]] and the [[Spider People]] — a Switzerland, both neutral, trading sea-lore for the paths through the [[Misty Mountains]]. The [[Dwarves|dwarves]], idle for five centuries — since the [[Bazaar]] rose, c. year 500, selling [[Star Metal|star metal]] there —, looked at the chaos, declared themselves the first and best of races, and walled [[Kharag Duun]] twice over: **the Great Wall** (Atlas). And Atlas made the Bazaar **untouchable** — neutral forever, selling bread and bedrolls to every army; no one fights inside it and no one wants to destroy it.

**c. year 620 — [[Great Battle of the Third Age|the Great Battle]].** Out of the burned mushroom forest spread a **[[Fungal Plague|fungal plague]]**: sterility in the acute phase, death untreated, passed by blood and saliva; worst in [[Glaive Port]], then the [[Bazaar]], then the island. The mushroom-folk, who breed by spores, are immune, and scholars think it the fungi's own defence ([[Svarog]]). [[Heart of the Plains]] raised **the [[Ballista Tower]]** — too big to aim, with a range of half the continent ([[Astralis]]) — and the [[Harpies|harpies]] founded **[[Divine Strike]]** to spot for it from the air ([[Atlas]]). [[Elyndra]], wounded, built a city on the coast below the forest of peace; what remained of the [[Ice Giants]] withdrew to [[Coldspire]], now rare, with few healers left. Glaive Port raised a third army, the [[Elf Outpost]] founded an order of **[[Elf Outpost Marines|marines]]** for war at sea, and the [[Dwarves|dwarves]] raised an army of their own. The [[Grove]] turned to magical defence — no walls, but a forest that shifts and scouts who walk in circles, spreading as small strange places rather than growing ([[Sylvanus]]). Famine came to Glaive Port as the fungus took livestock, fish and crops, and a fourth army rose. Astralis gave the [[Fire Genasi]] **the [[Ember Dancers]]**, a sect of dual-wielding blade dancers. The Grove raised **an army of [[Water Druids|water druids]]**. Then Glaive Port's four armies marched south for bread, against the Grove: **the Great Battle of the Third Age**. The druids confused them, their generals vanished — eaten by fungus or by trees — and with the [[Spider People]] they drove them out; the forest swallowed them. Fewer than half died. The rest scattered as bandits and marauders across a devastated wasteland, and the Grove survived, wounded. (The [[Fall of Glaive Port]] counts as the first great battle of the Age; this, the second.)

**c. year 630 — the [[Shattering]].** The dead of the battlefield rose: the plague made them **[[Fungal Zombies|fungal zombies]]**, mindless and leaderless, in every stage of decay ([[Astralis]]). The [[Lizardmen|lizardmen]] took up arms ([[Sylvanus]]). In [[Glaive Port]] a leper healed himself and began to heal others — **[[Melakai]] the Messenger**, [[Svarog]]'s avatar. [[Karros and Kassander]] led the armies of [[Heart of the Plains]] and [[Greenhaven]] against the [[Water Elves|water elves]] on their land; it ended even, and became a standing **[[War Zone]]** where [[Humans|humans]] and [[Centaurs|centaurs]] fight elves without end. The [[Hollow Knight]] raised a second lizardman army ([[Atlas]]). Sylvanus sent the lizardmen through the [[Underdark]] against the Tree; the [[Grove]]'s misdirection held, its assassins killed their chiefs, and the lizardmen turned on each other. The Hollow Knight built them a second war camp, and they split into two warring factions around the Underdark mouth. Melakai founded **the [[Mycomancers]]** — humans, [[Orcs|orcs]], anyone — who command the fungal dead; they became Glaive Port's ruling class, and the dead now patrol its streets without harming anyone (Svarog). The [[Deepkin]], true mushroom-folk, raised an army, since raising corpses with fungus is a travesty and it was their forest that burned (Astralis). Then Atlas, disgusted that war had become druids and misdirection instead of steel on steel, poured all his power into breaking magic itself. **One great crystal shattered** — the one on the [[Last Tide]]'s island, chosen, it is said, by chance — and **the rest cracked**. The cracks are still spreading, slowly, like sand through an hourglass; one day there may be no magic left. The surge ran across the whole continent, and plants, beasts and everything living twisted into **[[Monstrosities|monstrosities]]**. The world had no ogres before; now it does. Magic survives, but the nearer the broken crystal, the wilder it gets. [[Sylvariel]] decided the elves could no longer stay apart, and raised a water elf army against the lizardmen. Svarog, delighted by the chaos and annoyed not to understand it, founded **the [[Mages' Guild]]** at the [[Bazaar]], to study magic, write it all down, and buy up crystal shards, meteor fragments and curiosities. And the [[Stone Talus]] raised another [[Dwarves|dwarf]] army.

**c. year 640, and the end.** [[Melakai]] raised [[Glaive Port]] a defensive army. [[Astralis]], angry that [[Atlas]] would suppress magic, struck him in the eye: **[[Left Eye of Atlas|the Left Eye]] of Atlas** collapsed on one side and its waters flooded the [[War Zone]] into swamp. The armies stopped knowing friend from foe, and men who had been killing each other hauled each other out of the quicksand. The [[Dwarves|dwarves]], seeing [[Humans|humans]] become the dominant race, marched with the [[Stone Talus]] and took **[[Greenhaven]]** as its wardens — no massacre, nothing else changed (Atlas). [[Sylvanus]] hid **a [[Lost City|Lost City of Knowledge and History]]** somewhere in the former desert: empty, dangerous, perhaps haunted, perhaps guarded by old machines. Its keepers recorded the whole unabridged history of the world, and it may know where the lost crystals are; no one in the world knows where it is. [[Ardora]] had [[Cinderforge]]'s scholars ring their cracking crystal with **Pillars of Magic Harnessing**, tall coils that catch the leaking magic and feed it to the city ([[Svarog]]). The [[Deepkin]] laid siege to Glaive Port, and it held: a siege without end (Astralis). [[Elyndra]] gathered an army to open a front against the [[Lizardmen|lizardmen]]. Mycomancer agents slipped out of Glaive Port disguised as fungus, to seed hidden cells in other cities (Svarog). The dwarves became **master diplomats**, taught in their schools (Atlas). Elyndra and the [[Water Elves|water elves]] together took the [[Jungle Island]]'s [[Underdark]] mouth, and the lizardmen were broken but not destroyed (Sylvanus). The elves, who had always had it easy, grew arrogant and decadent, trampling the weak — the whole civilisation was corrupted (Svarog). [[Karros and Kassander]] came home from the front, found the dwarves ruling Greenhaven, and turned them to the honourable way (Astralis). But Atlas raised among those same dwarves a ruling caste, **the [[Magisters]]**, who now hold that dwarves are not only the first race but the best, and will do anything to stay in power. And the Age ended as it began, with **a meteor**. Svarog, god of chaos, let it fall where chaos chose, and it struck the mountain slopes beside the [[Ballista Tower|Ballista]]'s city and tore the glaciers off the highest peaks on the continent. The earthquake and mudslides broke the city, buried armies, made another swamp and a crater-lake, and cracked the Ballista. [[Sky Spear]]'s ancient ice split and drained, and the mountain is now porous — tunnels and caverns open where none were, and who knows what lives in them (Svarog).

*Every Age begins and ends with the fall of a meteor of unknown origin.*

*— So things stand. The campaign begins at the [[Bazaar]]. —*

## 3 · The world at the end of the Third Age

Where things stand as the campaign opens — a few months to a year after the [[Third Meteor|last meteor]].

- **The [[Bazaar]]** — neutral and untouchable, the one safe crossroads left, drowning in refugees. The [[Mages' Guild]] buys shards and curiosities here, the [[Kamatari]] lend money, and the plague has already been through. *The campaign starts here.*
- **[[Glaive Port]]** — mostly human; [[Orcs|orcs]] the nominal rulers, the [[Mycomancers]] the real ones, through religion. The [[Fungal Zombies|fungal dead]] patrol the streets. Besieged without end by the [[Deepkin]]. The [[Branded]] still hold its waters. [[Melakai]] lives here, and its four armies went out and died.
- **The [[Wasteland]]** east of the [[Grove]], along the road into the desert — [[Glaive Port]]'s scattered armies turned bandit, and [[Fungal Zombies|fungal zombies]] rising from the battlefield.
- **The [[War Zone]]** — the swamp where [[Left Eye of Atlas|the Left Eye]] of [[Atlas]] broke; [[Humans|humans]] and [[Centaurs|centaurs]] against [[Water Elves|water elves]], forever.
- **[[Greenhaven]]** — [[Dwarves|dwarf]]-ruled; purified to the honourable way by [[Karros and Kassander]], yet governed by the [[Magisters]]. Which wins remains to be seen.
- **[[Jungle Island]]** — the [[Lizardmen|lizardmen]] split in two and at war with each other; the island's war camps still stand, and the elves hold its [[Underdark]] mouth; across the water the remnants of the two armies that went through the Underdark camp at the mainland mouth, fighting each other.
- **The [[Last Tide]]'s island** — the shattered crystal. Wild magic and [[Monstrosities|monstrosities]]; a decadent, corrupted elf navy that can't build new ships for a generation.
- **[[Cinderforge]]** — harnessing its cracked crystal with pillars; the [[Cinderwing Cartel|Cartel]] grounded, chasing [[Portals|portals]] and meteor shards.
- **[[Kharag Duun]]** — walled twice over, full of diplomats and [[Star Metal|star metal]].
- **[[Sky Spear]]** — suddenly porous, with tunnels open that no one has walked.
- **Everywhere** — [[Monstrosities|monstrosities]]; Mycomancer cells hidden in the cities; the cracks in the crystals slowly spreading.

## 4 · Magic, meteors and time

- **Six [[Great Crystals|great crystals]]** fuel all magic, one per school. Four are known: **[[Elaruun]]** (the dragon's island, behind the winds) · **the [[Last Tide]]'s island** — *shattered* · **the [[Frozen North]]** near [[Coldspire]] — cracked · **[[Cinderforge]]** — cracked, now ringed by the Pillars of Magic Harnessing. **Two are lost** — no one knows where, not even the gods. One of them governs [[Portals|portals]]. The [[Lost City]] may know. Which school belongs to which crystal is not known.
- **The cracks are spreading**, like sand through an hourglass. Magic survives, but grows wilder the nearer you come to the broken crystal, and the surge twisted living things into [[Monstrosities|monstrosities]] across the continent.
- **[[Desert Crystals|Desert crystals]]** — small, obsidian-like, grown in the south-eastern sands; they amplify magic and are mined. The desert [[Orcs|orcs]] fused a great many into the obelisk called **the [[Final End]]**.
- **[[Portals]]** — possible only near **shards of the [[First Meteor|first meteor]]** ([[World's Breach]]); nearly impossible elsewhere. The [[Cinderwing Cartel|Cartel]] has mastered them. The [[Second Meteor|second meteor]]'s site (the [[First Fortress]]) has not been investigated.
- **[[Meteors]]** — *every Age begins and ends with the fall of a meteor of unknown origin.* Three have fallen: [[World's Breach]] (Age I), the [[First Fortress]] (Age II), the [[Ballista Tower|Ballista]]'s mountain (end of Age III).
- **The [[Skybreak]]** — magical storms close the sky; nothing flies.
- **Time** — years are counted from **year zero**, the fall of the second meteor on the [[First Fortress]]. The First Age lasted some 6,000 years before it; the Second some 600 years after it; the Third about fifty years, ended by the third meteor c. year 650.
- **No moon** yet, so no tides.
